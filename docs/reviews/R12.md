## Findings

### Errors

**E1. Spec 9.3 does not hold for designs with a photo: the colour seed and the type seed move the photo.** `src/engine/layout.ts` (the photo's role search runs inside `build` per form in `passesOnce`, ~line 172) with `tests/proof.test.ts:69` and `:81` (9.3 tests run without a photo). This is the builder's own new finding. It is real and wider than reported. A probe over 100 designs per style (test photo, stub measure) changed one seed and compared the photo's role and scale:
- colour seed alone: moved it in 1 of 100 Swiss and 4 Brutalist;
- type seed alone: moved it in 3 Swiss and 2 Brutalist;
- Postmodern and Vaporwave: 0 for both seeds.

Spec 9.3 says the colour seed leaves every box where it was and the type seed never moves a graphic, and the photo is a graphic. Cause: the photo's role is chosen per palette form (text over the photo reads contrast and the 'It shows' cover per form), and the 'It shows' cover counts the set glyphs, which the type seed changes. This predates R12 (R14), but R12's Layout-lock fix pins the symptom in the app without fixing it, and no test covers it.

Fix: choose the photo's role and placement once per candidate, before the colour pass. Take the first role that passes 'It shows' and rule 4 in every form, the same way the layer bans now work. Read the 'It shows' cover from type-seed-independent boxes (the layout's text zones), not from the set glyphs. Then extend the two 9.3 tests to run with the test photo in every role. If the cover cannot be made type-independent, Pedro or a Fable chat must write a 9.3 waiver for the photo into spec 8.9. Until one of these lands, it goes to Carried as an error, not as a note.

### Should-fix

**S1. Two more photocopy colours are mixed from two roles and still keep the palette's mix after a colour edit. The spec line now claims the whole class is handled.** These are the info label plate, `src/engine/parts/type/pomo-copy.ts:110` (`mixHex(paper, toner, -0.16)` / `mixHex(toner, paper, 0.06)`), and the caption's two faded copies, `:155` (`fadeTo(paper, toner, …)`). `recolour.ts mapItem` maps only palette role colours, so with paper and toner edited the label stays a tint of the old paper, and the faded caption copies stay at the old colours (rule 4 then repairs them with plates or ink swaps instead of mixing them again). Spec 5 (`docs/SPEC.md:96`) now reads "Colours a style mixes from two roles … are mixed again from the edited roles (R12)". Fix: record the recipe on rect and text items too: `mixed` for the label; `{a, b, ratio}` for the fades, so `fadeTo` runs again on the edited colours. Apply both in `mapItem` and test them with the photocopy edit test. Otherwise name the exceptions in the spec line and in Carried.

### Minor

**M1. The boxed-word test uses the code's own geometry as its oracle.** `tests/pomo.test.ts:240` judges with `inksMeet`, the function the push uses (`pomo-copy.ts:313-321`). A wrong `inksMeet` (for example, rotated boxes never meeting) would pass both. Smoke's pixel read covers it in practice. Fix: add a unit test of `inksMeet` on known pairs (axis-aligned touching and apart, 45° rotated corner overlap, grown by g).

**M2. The test hard-codes the halo.** `tests/pomo.test.ts` (in the new test, `reach = t.size * (0.06 + …)`) repeats `HALO` (`pomo-copy.ts:30`) as a literal. Fix: export `HALO` and import it, as `BOX_PAD` is.

**M3. The Layout lock turns the pinned role into an apparent hand pick.** `src/app/state.ts:66`, `src/app/App.tsx:261`. After a locked Shuffle, `photo.place.role` is set, so the Advanced Photo role control shows a chosen value instead of Auto showing what the design draws (spec 10.3). The pin also switches off the 'It shows' next-role fallback (spec 8.9, "a role pinned in Advanced"), so later locked Shuffles that change graphics or text over the photo can leave it under 35% showing. Fix: mark the hold (for example `place.held: true`), let the Photo tab show Auto while it is set, and clear it on unlock. Or say in spec 8.9 that the lock pins the role as a pick, with those effects.

**M4. Every locked Shuffle with a photo pays for one extra full solve.** `src/app/App.tsx:261` calls `sceneOf` again before shuffling. The stage's `scene` is already that Scene when its spec equals `s`. Fix: use `scene` when it was drawn from `s`, otherwise solve.

**M5. The smoke's `boxed` flag guesses which word is boxed.** `src/engine/pixels.ts:287` marks as boxed any title-slot text with no halo stroke. Unhaloed extra title layers would be mislabelled. Fix: mark the boxed word on its item (for example `part === 'word' + boxed`, or a `boxed: true` field) and read that. Every format now reads 0 cells under their ratio before the pass, so smoke can also fail on any `before` cell, not only boxed ones (`tools/smoke.py:179`, `:198`).

**M6. The commit records nothing about what it left, and two items are not gated on Pedro, the spec or Google Fonts.** Row R12 has no Done-when; it "works every note and everything in Carried". Most Carried items left are taste calls for Pedro (R12 from R5, R6, R9, bundle 4, bundle 5, R17 ×3, R14 spec), spec questions (R10 from R8; margin, case, second graphic) or need Google Fonts (R3). Those are left for a stated reason. Two items are not:
- R10 (from R10) tracking: Brutalist, Postmodern and Vaporwave read no tracking. That is engineering plus a range from 9.2, with no Pedro gate in its text.
- R12 (from R8) item 1: a Brutalist design gets two marks instead of three, an engine defect.

Fix at the close: record in the changelog why each was left, or re-target them to a row. Record E1 in Carried.
