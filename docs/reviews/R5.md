### Should-fix

- **S1. The title shrinks by more than 25% on tilted full-bleed, centred-low and 1:1 split pages, and the test checks only graphic-below.** `tests/swiss.test.ts:197` forces `pick.layout = 'graphic-below'`, so "a tilted or turned layout keeps its title within 25% of the upright size" is tested for one of eight layouts. Measured over 30 seed sets with `layout.size: normal` (tilted against upright, same seeds): 1:1 full-bleed tilt worst 0.63, mean 0.68; 9:16 centred-low tilt worst 0.67, mean 0.70; 3:4 centred-low tilt worst 0.70; 1:1 split tilt worst 0.73. Specs: swiss 1:1 light, seeds `{layout: 1084, colour: 2012, graphic: 3012, type: 4012}`, `pick.layout: "full-bleed"`, `params: {"layout.size": "normal"}`, title 140 px; add `"layout.transform": "tilt"` and it is 88 px. The same seeds at 9:16 with `centred-low`: 162 px against 108 px. README's "with the title kept at size" overstates it. Fix: loop the test over every layout in `SWISS_LAYOUTS` and every format (skip vertical with quarter); then on a turned page let the full-bleed panel and text box take the width the page has (`parts/layout/swiss.ts:84-88`: panel `w: M + mod * 5`, text `mod * 5 - M * 0.6`, both on the smaller page) and drop centred-low's one-module side indent and shrink its graphic share (`:110-116`), until the test passes.

- **S2. The tuning rule was loosened in a way that hides clumsy designs.** Round 4 to 5 (`docs/tuning-R5.md`) took the flagged count from 23 to 4 by changing the rule, not the designs. Leaving the 9:16 story zones out is sound (they belong to the story frame). Counting every non-plate rect over a tenth of the canvas as cover is not: on the centred-low and split layouts the field is the whole page or half of it, so the `empty` rule can never fire there however little sits on the field. Graphic boxes also count as covered whatever their ink, so a box of a few crosshair specks counts as full. Type only is exempt. That is why `small graphic` and `tiny title` never fired in any round, and why the designs below are not flagged (`src/engine/proof.ts:224-247`). Legal but clumsy, seen by eye:
  - centred-low tilt, small text block in a large bare field, bare foot: swiss, 9:16, dark, seeds `{layout: 202128586, colour: 959051492, graphic: 651549249, type: 304502735}`, `params: {"layout.transform": "tilt"}`; and 9:16 light `{1084, 2012, 3012, 4012}`, `pick.layout: "centred-low"`, `params: {"layout.transform": "tilt"}` (bottom third bare).
  - split tilt, a sparse crosshairs graphic in a half page of yellow: swiss, 9:16, light, `{layout: 1090580831, colour: 1836196521, graphic: 1261399322, type: 407811517}`, `params: {"layout.transform": "tilt"}`.
  - graphic-below, nine crosshair specks in a bare lower half, the lattice cut-in nearly invisible: swiss, 1:1, light, `{layout: 53186664, colour: 227418004, graphic: 184198447, type: 2107270755}`, no params.
  - type only: the title stops at its start size (243 px, about 62% of the width), and the lower 45% of the page is bare between the subtitle and the info: swiss, 3:4, light, `{layout: 4242, colour: 1, graphic: 5151, type: 6}` (every cell of `shots/palettes-swiss.jpg`).

  Fix: count a field as cover only where a graphic or text sits on it; weight a graphic box by a rough ink density (the sparse graphics: crosshairs at high count, rings and lattice at hairline weight, rules at low count) or sample the rendered pixels against the ground colour; keep type only in the rule with its own threshold. Rerun `tools/tune.py swiss 300`, fix what it then lists (for type only: let the oversize title grow until the width or the room binds, or send it low), and record the corrected rule and the new counts in `docs/tuning-R5.md`.

- **S3. The numeral is clipped flat by its own box, the disc fault again.** `src/engine/graphics/numeral.ts:24-28`: `scale` reaches 1.3 of the box height ("over 1 bleeds"), but the box clips, so the figure is cut straight at the box edge inside the page, not at the canvas edge. `shots/sheet-swiss-light.jpg` and `-dark.jpg`, third cell (9:16 orange): a "0" cut flat at the top of its box, reading as a barrel, which is what bundle 1 S2 rejected for the disc. Also swiss, 1:1, dark, `{layout: 770938808, colour: 1655225195, graphic: 123597417, type: 441580073}` (a "2" cut into a fragment). Fix: cap `scale` at 1, or let it exceed 1 only on the sides where the box meets the canvas edge (as the disc now does), and add a range-end test like the disc's.

- **S4. Swiss has no snapshot hash.** `tests/snapshot.test.ts:13` leaves Swiss out "because R5 tunes it". R5 is the row that finishes Swiss, and Pedro judges it before bundle 3, which will touch shared code (`layout.ts`, `parts/frame.ts`, the graphics library, `items`). Without a hash, a later row can move Swiss silently. Fix: add `swiss: '<hash>'` from `RSTR_SNAPSHOT_ALL=1`, after the fixes here, and update the comment.

- **S5. The tuning rules have no tests.** `clumsyOf` and `runTune` (`src/engine/proof.ts:200-264`) are logic without tests, and the row's tightening is measured by them. Fix: unit tests on hand-built Scenes: a page of bare ground flags `empty`; story-zone ground at 9:16 does not count; a full field with a small graphic flags (after S2); a three-word title under 6% of the width flags `tiny title`; a fallback flags.

- **S6. The blocks graphic's own disc collides with a disc cut-in.** When the first graphic is `blocks` and a cut-in is a disc, the cut-in's disc lands on or beside the blocks disc and leaves a crescent of it showing, an eclipse that reads as a mistake. swiss, 3:4, light, `{layout: 24938, colour: 218658, graphic: 2608718, type: 9462}` (centred-low, blocks + waves), and the second design still flagged in `docs/tuning-R5.md` (3:4 dark `{1377348019, 2913652576, 2126723483, 2221845038}`, blocks + dots). Fix in `parts/graphics/swiss.ts:100-103`: with `blocks` first, place a disc cut-in away from the largest cell, or set the blocks disc to 0 when a disc is cut in.

- **S7. Crosshairs shrink to specks at high counts.** `src/engine/graphics/crosshairs.ts:16`: arm length is `size / sqrt(count)` of the short side, so at 16 marks the minimum arm is under 4% of the box. This is the R1 to R5 "a graphic that is too small to read" case, and the low-end fix (0.06 to 0.15) does not reach it. Seed in S2 (1:1 light `{53186664, ...}`). Fix: a floor on the arm of about half a module (`mod` is in the params), or cap `count` by how many modules the box holds.

### Minor

- **M1. `shots/palettes-swiss.jpg` now shows only type only.** Its fixed seeds (`tools/sheet.py:54`, layout 4242) roll type only after R5, so the sheet no longer shows any palette with a graphic, field or disc (the sheet bundle 1 S2 was judged on). Fix: for Swiss pin `pick.layout: "graphic-above"` (or `full-bleed`) on the palette sheet.
- **M2. The transform-forced smoke loop does not check fallbacks.** `tools/smoke.py:65-70` prints fallbacks but never calls `fallback_check`, so a tilted page that always fell back would pass. Fix: `fallback_check(f'9.2 swiss {fmt} {tr}', r, 0)`.
- **M3. The field-ink test claims more than it checks.** `tests/swiss.test.ts:217` is named "takes an ink that passes 4.5:1" but asserts only "not fallback", and does not check that the picked layout is the one drawn. Fix: assert `sc.frame === layout`, and that each text item's fill against the field colour reaches `needRatio(px)`, or rename it.
- **M4. Info and caption at the head invert the spec's rule order.** Spec 8.1: info above a rule, caption under the rule. With both at the head, `parts/type/swiss.ts:96-97` stacks caption, rule, then info, so the info sits under the rule. Fix: at the head use the foot order (info, rule, caption); keep caption-over-rule only when the caption alone is at the head.
- **M5. Single-fill blocks patterns read as half-empty in large boxes.** `FILLS` entries 6 to 9 and 11 (`src/engine/graphics/blocks.ts:20-21`) fill one cell of three, which leaves the vertical-layout box mostly ground (both designs still flagged in `docs/tuning-R5.md`). Fix: keep them for pins and Advanced, and roll only the two-fill patterns when the box is over about a third of the page.
