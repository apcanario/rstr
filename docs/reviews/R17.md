## Errors

**E1. Two layers of one level are not set alike (spec 8.6), and a second Brutalist headline breaks 8.3.**
- Where: `src/engine/parts/shared/layers.ts:95` (`placeLayers`), the `*_LEVELS` tables in `src/engine/parts/type/{swiss,pomo,brutal,vapor}.ts`, and `docs/SPEC.md:250`.
- What 8.6 says: "Two layers of the same level are set alike and kept apart." That line was settled.
- What was built: the lead layer is set by the style's slot rules and every later layer of the same level by the shared placer. The two look nothing alike. Probe (c), lead headline against a second headline, 40 seeds each:
  - Brutalist: the extra is 0.31 x the lead's size and never full width. With seed 700 the lead is 375 px across 982 px and the extra is 108 px across 234 px. This also breaks 8.3, "Every line is scaled to fill the full width".
  - Postmodern: the lead is tilted strips or tiles with hard shadows; the extra is plain text on a flat plate, in another family in 11 of 40.
  - Vaporwave: the lead has the chrome fill with an outline and echoes; the extra is flat ink in a window box.
  - Swiss: the extra is 0.57 x the lead.
- What the sheets show: `layers-brutal.jpg` cells 1 and 3, `layers-pomo.jpg` cells 2 and 4, and `layers-vapor.jpg` cells 1 and 3 read as the real title followed by a plain list of other titles.
- The test `tests/layers.test.ts:178` ("set alike") only compares two extras (body against body), so it cannot see this.
- Fix:
  1. Set every layer of a level through the style's own treatment of that level. A second headline, or a second subhead, data or caption, goes through the type part's own setter with the lead's face, case, fill treatment and size class, in a place the style allows. Concretely: Brutalist adds the extra headline as further full-width stretched lines of the title block, or as a second full-width block. Ice cream sets it as strips, photocopy as collided words, Vaporwave in chrome.
  2. The shared placer may keep body and label, which have no slot rules.
  3. Replace the second half of the 8.6 "Built at R17" line so it no longer contradicts the settled line.
  4. Add a test comparing lead and extra of the same level per style: same family, same case, size within 0.8 to 1.25 of the lead, and for Brutalist every line at the full width.

**E2. The 9.2 run over layers does not cover counts against formats and modes.**
- Where: `src/engine/proof.ts:461` (`s.format = formats[i % formats.length]; s.mode = Math.floor(i / formats.length) % 2 ...`) with `layerMix` at `:441` (`n = 1 + (i % 8)`).
- What happens: because `i % 4` and `i % 8` are tied, probe (b) finds exactly 8 combinations across 200 seeds: 1 layer in 1:1 light, 2 in 3:4 light, 3 in 9:16 light, 4 in A light, then 5 to 8 in the same formats in dark.
- Consequences:
  - 8 layers are never tried in 9:16, the smallest text area.
  - No list of 1 to 4 layers is tried in dark.
  - Smoke (`tools/smoke.py:67`) calls the same function, so the real-font run has the same hole.
  - The Done-when line is marked tested, but the test would not fail if 9.2 broke for 8 layers in 9:16.
- Fix:
  1. Take format and mode from other bits of `i`, for example `format = FORMAT_LIST[Math.floor(i / 8) % 4]` and `mode = Math.floor(i / 32) % 2`, so each block of 64 covers every count x format x mode.
  2. Add an assertion in `tests/layers.test.ts:84` that all 64 combinations occur.
  3. In line with 9.2 ("every style x format x mode, over 200 seed sets"), run 200 lists per format in smoke, not 200 per style.

## Should-fix

**S1. "The lowest levels shrink first" does not hold between lead layers and extras.**
- Where: `src/engine/parts/shared/layers.ts:144` and `src/engine/layout.ts:194`.
- What happens: extras are placed only in the room the leads leave. A higher-level extra, such as a second headline or a second subhead, is flagged while a lower-level lead, such as the caption, is drawn at full size. Probe (d), a crowded 9:16 list (headline, subhead, data, caption, second subhead, second headline): this happens in 11 of 60 Brutalist designs and 1 of 60 Swiss designs.
- Spec 8.6: "If the layers cannot all fit legally, the lowest levels shrink first."
- Fix:
  1. In `solve`, weigh flagged layers by level (`LEVEL_RANK`) rather than counting them, so a candidate that flags a caption beats one that flags a headline.
  2. Let a flagged higher extra take the room of a lower lead, for example by setting the caption and label leads through the placer when an extra above them is flagged.
  3. Test: in crowded lists, no layer is flagged while a layer of a lower level is drawn at more than its minimum.

**S2. Vaporwave extras are placed on the sun.**
- Where: `src/engine/parts/shared/layers.ts:105-111`. Only the ground and the passes are skipped from `soft`, and the second pass of `find` ignores `soft`.
- Spec 8.4: "The sun is the one graphic that does not keep a halo off the type; layout keeps type off it."
- Seen in `layers-vapor.jpg`:
  - cell 4 (A, 8 layers): plates for "SIGNAL" and "EIGHT PLAIN WORDS…" over the sun;
  - cell 9 (1:1, 5 layers, seeds 354/454/554/6): "Generated, not drawn" on a plate in the middle of the sun.
- Fix: the Vaporwave type part names the graphics a layer must never cover (the sun and its cut-ins). `placeLayers` puts those in `hard`. Add a test that no extra's quad overlaps the sun's.

**S3. Extras on ground plates cut across graphics (legal but clumsy).**
- Where: `src/engine/parts/shared/layers.ts:155-164`. The placer stops looking clear of the graphics once the size drops under 0.75 of the start, then places over them.
- Seen in:
  - `layers-swiss.jpg` cell 2 (A, 4 layers, seeds 301/401/501/6): three ground-colour slabs cut through the diagonal stripes;
  - `layers-swiss.jpg` cell 4 (A, 8 layers, seeds 307/407/507/6): plates over the pink rings;
  - `layers-vapor.jpg` cells 3 and 4: a stack of white windows over the sky and the floor.
- In Swiss, a plate of ground across a graphic is only a ref look in the full-bleed layout (8.1).
- Fix:
  1. Look clear of the graphics down to the level's minimum before going over them.
  2. Go over a graphic only where the style draws text on a plate itself (Swiss full-bleed, Vaporwave windows, photocopy paper strips). Elsewhere, flag the layer rather than slab it across a graphic. Or reserve more room (S5).
  3. Re-look at the cells above.

**S4. Positions are only offered for the lead layer (spec 10.3).**
- Where: `src/app/App.tsx:688-691` (`lead?.id === l.id && ...`).
- Spec 10.3, Text tab: "Each slot or layer: field, on/off, position". Spec 8.6: "Each style maps every level to … the places it may go." Extras have neither a position control nor a choice of places (only `from: top | bottom`).
- Fix: give `LevelSet` a list of named places per level per style, read `layer.pos` in `placeLayers`, and show the position control on every layer row in Advanced. If the builder thinks that is out of scope, it needs a Carried item for Pedro or a Fable chat; the "Built at R17" line cannot decide it.

**S5. Brutalist flags about 7% of layers, and half of a full A design.**
- Where: `src/engine/parts/shared/layers.ts:195` (`roomFor`) and the Brutalist stretch in `src/engine/parts/layout/brutal.ts`.
- Evidence:
  - Smoke with the bundled fonts: 64 of 900 flagged. The stub measure gives 59 of 900.
  - `layers-brutal.jpg` cell 4 (A, 8 layers, seeds 307/407/507/6) flags t5 to t8: the title is stretched over the whole page.
  - Cell 10 (A, 8 layers, seeds 363/463/563/6) flags t3 and t8.
- The unit test allows up to 12% (`tests/layers.test.ts:89`), so it would not notice the share doubling.
- Fix:
  1. With extras, let Brutalist's size class and stretch keep the reserved room. Never stretch the title into it; prefer `small` or `normal` over `oversize` when extras exist.
  2. Lower the test ceiling per style to what the fixed run gives plus a margin, for example 3%.

**S6. The 9.2 run over layers checks the Scene only.**
- Where: `tools/smoke.py:67`.
- What it does: the run uses marks off, Clean, no photo and Postmodern's default dialect.
- What it misses, though each touches the new text items:
  - the copier-pass cell check;
  - the finish 3:1 guard (7.1);
  - marks with layers (marks keep off "text items"; the containers now skip `extra` items, `src/engine/marks.ts:298,324,338`);
  - the photo's "It shows" floor (8.9), when up to four more plates sit on the page.
- Fix: in smoke, run the layer lists (at least 50 per style and format) through the marks light and full run, the finish proof, the copier proof (photocopy) and the photo run.

**S7. The flagged-row check in the app is usually vacuous.**
- Where: `tools/appcheck.py:417-425`.
- What it checks: marked rows equal `scene.flagged`. With `EIGHT` at 3:4, probe (e) finds something flagged in 0 of 50 Postmodern and Vaporwave seeds, 6 of 50 Swiss and 11 of 50 Brutalist, so the check mostly compares two empty sets.
- Fix:
  1. Load a list that always flags, such as the eight long headlines on 1:1 of `tests/layers.test.ts:94`.
  2. Assert that at least one row carries `data-flagged` and that its level button reads `… !`.

## Minor

**M1. The wordmark's tracking is loose in lower case.** `src/app/app.css:12` (`letter-spacing: 0.22em`). It was right for capitals; in lower case `r s t r` reads as four spaced letters (`shots/screen-360x640-text-1.png`). Fix: about 0.06em, and look at both screen sizes.

**M2. The text field got narrow at 360 px.** Level button (56), switch (52) and More (48) leave the field about 120 CSS px. It shows "Signal and n" and "A modular" (`shots/screen-360x640-text-1.png`), against the wide field of `refs/ui-2-text.jpg`. Fix: move on/off into the More row, or make the level button 48 px and drop the More button's padding, so the field keeps at least 160 px at 360. `src/app/App.tsx:484-490`, `src/app/app.css:81`.

**M3. The placer's memo key does not include every input.** `src/engine/parts/shared/layers.ts:112`. The key leaves out the measure (fonts loaded or not), the plan's transform (`plan.xf`) and `spec.params`. It holds only while those are implied by the seeds, `k` and the rounded rects. A stale hit is caught by `check` for a candidate but not for the fallback. Fix: add `plan.xf` (angle, origin), a measure id and `spec.params` to the key, or drop the cache.

**M4. The spec line does not describe the Text tab that was built.** `docs/SPEC.md:250` says "an Add row with one button per level" and "in Advanced, Move up". The build has one level cycler plus one Add button, and a More button that opens Move up, Move down, Remove and Done. Fix: make the line say what was built.

**M5. A test can pass without testing anything.** `tests/layers.test.ts:125` ("a v1 design shortened in its fallback flags…") asserts one branch or the other, so it passes whether or not anything is shortened. Fix: pick a spec that is shortened under the stub measure, assert that it is, then assert the flag.

**M6. No screen shows 8 layers or a row's edit controls.** `tools/sheet.py:252` saves the Text tab only with the default four layers, so the sheets never show 8 layers, a flagged row or a row's edit controls. Fix: in the screen pass, load `EIGHT` (one page per screen size, Simple and Advanced) and open one row's More.

**M7. A clumsy design.** `layers-swiss.jpg` cell 9 (1:1, 5 layers, seeds 354/454/554/6): the extras huddle small at the top right over an empty page. The cause is the same as S3/S5: room is kept only "at the foot", and every level starts from the same end. This goes to Pedro with the sheets.

## Verdict

**Fix.** 2 errors, 7 should-fix, 7 minor.

Migration, the 9.2 run (where it runs), flagging on the Scene, the Text tab at 8 layers and the lower-case wordmark hold. E1 changes how a second layer of a level is set, not the layer model, so it is a fix, not a rebuild.
