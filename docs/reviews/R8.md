## The builder's spec edits (8.3) and the marks change

- **Words joining onto fewer lines when height binds:** does not contradict a settled decision. Spec 8.3 asks for one word per line, every line at full width, and a stretch of at least 0.55. Where all three cannot hold, one must give. Joining keeps "full width", the line the spec bolds, and review R1 M4 asked for exactly that. The typed break kept as typed is spec 8.5. Acceptable, but the code joins far more often than "where height binds" (E1).
- **Size classes:** spec 8.0 lets each style say what small, normal and oversize mean, so the shares are the builder's to set. The clause "a small title may join words to leave the band its room" is different. It lets the band beat the settled "one word per line up to four words" when the height does not bind. That is a change to a settled rule, made by a builder (S2).
- **A free strip at the canvas edges with marks on:** no contradiction. Spec 7.2 places marks "in free space only", and a Brutalist page has none. It does mean turning Marks on moves the poster's text, which no other style does (M2).
- **Eight layouts listed:** spec 8.0 asks for eight; the slab is new and fits 8.3's "big band" language. Fine.
- **Cropped title not built:** the reason holds. Spec 8.3 wants it, but no rule waives 9.2 rule 1 for it. Spec 8.2 has such a waiver for the paper edge; this has none. Deferring it is honest, but nothing in the backlog carries it yet (S3).
- **Underline bar rule in `src/engine/marks.ts`, every style:** it fixes the Brutalist slit, but it also drops the bar from half of Vaporwave's full-marks designs. It also lets Swiss's bar hang from the right end of a flush-left title (S1, M1).

## Findings

### Errors

**E1. One word per line, the rule the refs are built on, is now the exception, even at normal size.** Spec 8.3: "One word per line up to four words". Spec 8.3 as the builder edited it allows joining only where full-width lines "cannot fit the height even at 0.55", or for a small title. It also says "Normal keeps one word per line where that leaves a band". Measured on the stub measure, the share of 2-to-4-word titles set on fewer lines than words:
- normal: title-top 20 of 24, title-bottom 18 of 30, table-first 22 of 36, split 12 of 12, boxed 8 of 18, title-up 20 of 24;
- oversize, title-up: 10 of 12;
- small: 50% to 100% by layout.

Real fonts show the same. `shots/sheet-brutal-light.jpg`: 9 of 12 cells read "SIGNAL / AND NOISE" or "SIGNAL AND NOISE"; only 2 are one word per line. `shots/variety-brutal.jpg`: 6 of 24. All three refs are "SIGNAL / AND / NOISE".

Cause:
- `src/engine/parts/layout/brutal.ts:115`: `ruleH` takes `Math.min` over the bundled faces. The band is sized so that only the *shortest* face keeps one word per line. Any other face (Anton, the default display, is not the shortest) gets a title box too low for its rule lines. `brutalFit` (`shared/brutal.ts`) then falls through to a joined break.
- `:120`: a small title is sized from `minH`, the joined minimum, on purpose.
- `:150-153`: the split sizes both halves from `minH` too, so the top half is always joined.
- `:88-90`: title-up caps the strip at 0.55 of the inner width, below what three one-word lines need at 0.55 stretch, so it joins too.

Legal but clumsy, the spec that makes it: `sheet-brutal-light.jpg` cell 2, brutal 3:4 light, seeds `{layout: 8919, colour: 106729, graphic: 1302709, type: 4031}`, riot, br-anton. "SIGNAL / AND NOISE" sits under a band (pink slab and disc) that takes a third of the page. The title could have kept three lines at a stretch above 0.55.

Fix:
- (a) `ruleH` takes `Math.max` over `REF_FACES`, so normal and oversize leave every bundled face its rule lines.
- (b) Small, the split (`:128`, `:150-153`) and title-up (`:88-90`, allow the strip to `0.7 * iw` before joining) size from `ruleH` first. They fall back to `minH` only where `ruleH` leaves no band of `BAND`.
- (c) Add a test: over `specs({styles: ['brutal'], n: 200, variants: 'cycle'})`, every non-fallback design with a 2-to-4-word title in normal or oversize keeps one word per line, with every core display face. Then require one word per line in at least 70% of all such designs.
- (d) Regenerate `variety-brutal.jpg` and compare it with `refs/brutal-*.jpg` without the pins.

### Should-fix

**S1. The new underline-bar rule halves the bar in Vaporwave and thins it in Swiss.** `src/engine/marks.ts:337` skips a bar whenever any graphic touches the 24 px `away` strip without touching the bar. It was meant for the Brutalist slit (Carried R8 from R7), but Vaporwave's sun, grid and stars touch that strip almost everywhere.

Underline bars with marks full, 180 designs per style (n 60, every format, cycle variants, light), base → build:

| Style | Base | Build |
|---|---|---|
| Swiss | 87 | 76 |
| Postmodern | 77 | 77 |
| Brutalist | 41 | 18 |
| Vaporwave | 108 | 51 |

Over the four styles that is 313 of 720 (43%) before and 222 of 720 (31%) after. `tests/marks.test.ts:102` asks for 30% overall, so it passes by one point. `shots/marks-full.jpg`: Vaporwave 1:1 and 3:4 have no bar. Spec 7.2: "full (also the containers: ... the title gets an underline bar and a micro label)".

Fix: skip only a real slit. Skip the bar when a graphic's or plate's near edge lies inside the `away` strip, with its far side beyond it. Do not skip when the bar and its strip lie over the graphic's body (sky, grid floor), where the old rule placed it. Then make the marks test per style: at least the base count less 10% for Swiss, Postmodern and Vaporwave. Brutalist keeps its own floor (the Carried item accepts no bar where there is no room).

**S2. The size-class clause lets a builder overrule a settled rule.** `docs/SPEC.md:206`: "a small title may join words to leave the band its room". Spec 8.3 line 1 (settled, CLAUDE.md: "do not reopen") makes one word per line the rule, broken only where the spec's own constraints conflict. Joining to make room for a graphic is a new trade, not a conflict. It is the trade behind E1.

Fix: strike the clause. Small means "about half of the area at one word per line, stretch nearer 0.55". If one word per line cannot leave a band, the band is thin or absent, as with normal. If small-joins is wanted, it is Pedro's call: put it to him under By hand with two sheets, joined and not, and record his answer. Do not keep it in the spec on the builder's word.

**S3. The cropped-title layout is deferred with no Carried item and no rule for it.** `docs/SPEC.md:211` says it is not built until R10, but `docs/BACKLOG.md` carries nothing for it. When R10 builds it, it needs a 9.2 rule 1 waiver that the spec does not have. Spec 8.2 grants one, in words, for the paper edge.

Fix at the close: add "R10 (from R8). The cropped title of spec 8.3 (Advanced only, off by default) is not built; it needs a rule 1 waiver for the title, written into spec 8.3 by Pedro or a Fable chat, before R10 builds it" to Carried, and name it in the R8 changelog entry.

**S4. Heavy asterisks draw as solid polygons, not asterisks.** `src/engine/parts/graphics/brutal.ts:27` moves `asterisks.weight` into the top 45% of 0.06 to 0.4, an arm half-thickness of 0.25 to 0.4 of the radius. At four to six arms the arms fill the whole star, which draws as a black 10- or 12-gon. Spec 8.3: "a row of asterisks".

Seen in:
- `shots/text-brutal.jpg` cells 4 to 6 (8 words, seeds `{layout: 78, colour: 89, graphic: 100, type: 5}`): a row of black polygons;
- `shots/variety-brutal.jpg` cell 13 (split, asterisks+lattice, 3:4 dark, seeds `{104128, 1265948, 15605808, 9772}`): yellow octagons;
- the white "discs" in the 1-word cells.

Fix: cap the heavy end so `arms * weight <= 0.7` (for example map heavy weight into 0.14 to 0.24, and fewer arms when weight is high). Test that an asterisk's filled share of its circle stays under 0.75 at every heavy draw.

**S5. A cut-in on a plate of the first graphic's own ink merges with it into one blob.** `src/engine/parts/graphics/brutal.ts:66` filters the cut-in roles only against the first graphic's plate (`under`), not against the colour the first graphic is drawn in (`C1.ink`). An ink disc cut into black arrows makes one black mass, with the arrow heads poking out of it.

`shots/text-brutal.jpg` cells 1 to 3 (1 word, seeds `{layout: 77, colour: 88, graphic: 99, type: 5}`, 3:4 clearest): the disc of white asterisks on ink sits over the black arrows and reads as one shape. Spec 8.0: "cut a second graphic into the first ... on a plate", meaning a plate that shows as a cut.

Fix: exclude from `roles` any role whose colour equals `coloursOn(P, under).ink` where the cut overlaps the first graphic (every cut does). Fall back to the accent. Test over 600 draws that no cut's plate colour equals the first graphic's ink.

### Minor

**M1. Swiss's underline bar may now hang from the right end of a flush-left title.** `src/engine/marks.ts:333` adds `at(T.x + T.w - len)` positions for every style: 7 of 76 Swiss bars in the build against 1 of 87 before. Spec 8.1: flush left, one axis. Fix: for Swiss (or for any title set flush left), try the left positions only.

**M2. Turning Marks on moves the Brutalist text.** `src/engine/parts/layout/brutal.ts:66-72` shrinks the page by `0.04 W` at both ends (or both sides on a quarter turn) whenever marks are on. Legal, and needed for three marks. But in every other style marks are a layer over a fixed design, and Pedro will see the title shrink and move when he flips Marks. Fix: say so in the R8 changelog as a choice. Ask Pedro under H2 whether it bothers him, rather than changing it now.

**M3. A tilted Brutalist page leaves wide empty corners and a smaller title.** This is the Swiss weakness of spec 8.1, not fixed here. Legal but clumsy:
- `shots/variety-brutal.jpg` cell 11, title-top tilt, 3:4 light, seeds `{layout: 88290, colour: 1056490, graphic: 13006390, type: 9710}`;
- cell 3, screen tilt, `{24938, 218658, 2608718, 9462}`.

Spec 8.0 depth: items "bleed off the canvas". Fix: on a tilt, grow the page frame by the tilt's corner loss for the band and the accent bar, so ground-colour corners are covered by the band or the bars, and keep the title box as is.

**M4. A tiny numeral reads as stray digits.** `shots/variety-brutal.jpg` cell 16, slab tilt, rules+numeral, 3:4 light, seeds `{127885, 1580135, 19504935, 9865}`: "234" sits small at the top right beside the accent bar. Spec 8.3: "a huge numeral". Fix: in `HEAVY` (`graphics/brutal.ts:30`) give `numeral` a floor on its glyph height (at least 0.6 of the band's height), or leave the numeral out of cut-ins narrower than that.

**M5. The layout header states shares the code does not use.** `src/engine/parts/layout/brutal.ts:6` says "small 55%, normal 75%"; `SHARE` at `:26` is 0.5 and 0.7, as spec 8.3 now says. Fix the comment.

**M6. A stale comment in smoke.** `tools/smoke.py:10`: "the rest report until R8, R9". Fix: "until R9".

**M7. Full width and the joining are proved only for the bundled faces.** The band is sized from the core display faces (`REF_FACES`). Smoke drew 8 of 10 Brutalist pairings with the default offline. A narrower fetched face (League Gothic, Big Shoulders, Bebas) needs a taller box for the same lines and may join more, or fall back where the bundled faces did not. Fix: add to Carried R3, item (1): "and for Brutalist, every pairing keeps every title line at full width with no fallback in the unpicked run, and the share of one-word-per-line titles is reported per pairing".

**M8. README not yet updated.** `README.md:7` and the Status table still list Brutalist as queued. Fix: at the close, add what Brutalist now does (eight layouts, size classes, the band, twelve graphics with the arrow, the free strip with marks on) and set bundle 4's status.

## Verdict

**Fix.** 1 error, 5 should-fix, 8 minor. Every "(tested)" Done-when line is true and has a test that would fail if it were false; check and smoke pass. Two things stop a pass:
- The full-width rule is met mostly by joining words, which breaks the settled one-word-per-line rule and the refs' look. The builder's own spec text ("normal keeps one word per line") is not true of the build.
- The marks change halves Vaporwave's underline bar.

Neither needs a rebuild: E1 is a change in how the band is sized (`ruleH`, the small, split and title-up paths), and S1 is one condition in `marks.ts`.
