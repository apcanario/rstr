## Findings

### Errors

**E1. Spec 10.3 narrowed by the builder: five listed parameters are written into the spec as "Not built", on a reason that does not hold for most of them.** `docs/SPEC.md:386` (added by this build). Spec 10.3's table lists margin, graphic share, horizon (Layout), title weight and tracking (Type); row R10 says "Spec 10.3 ... controls generated from the parts' parameter lists" and its Done-when says "every parameter of every style can be set and changes the design (tested)". The builder wrote "Not built, because no part reads such a value yet" into the spec itself. `CLAUDE.md` says decisions in the spec are settled and not reopened; `RUN.md` 2 lets a builder edit the spec only for a Pedro note that contradicts it. There was no note. The reason is circular (R10 is the row that makes the parts read them) and is false in substance for three of the five:
- horizon: Vaporwave already draws it from the layout stream within 56% to 64% (`src/engine/parts/layout/vapor.ts:34`, spec 8.4); `layout.horizon` is the same few lines as `layout.size`.
- graphic share: Swiss computes it as `gshare` (`src/engine/parts/layout/swiss.ts:84`) and the Swiss `band` draw (`:58`); Brutalist's band share comes from the size class.
- tracking: the measure and fit already take tracking in ems (`src/engine/text.ts:5`, `:30`, `:46`).
- title weight needs faces that are loaded (spec 6, "never lay out with the wrong font"), and a margin control runs against style rules (8.1 "one margin, 72 px"; 8.3 "about 48 px"). Those two are fair to question, but the place for that is Carried for Pedro or a Fable chat, not a spec edit by the builder.
So the Done-when line "every parameter of every style can be set" is not true.
Fix: take the "Not built..." sentence out of `docs/SPEC.md:386`. Build `layout.horizon` (Vaporwave horizon layouts, 0.56 to 0.64), `layout.share` (Swiss `gshare`, Brutalist band), `type.track` (where the type part sets the title's tracking, range narrowed until 9.2 passes in every layout), and title weight where the pairing names more than one weight of the display family, with the 4 s font wait. If margin is left out, say why in a Carried item (R10, to Pedro/Fable), not in the spec.

**E2. Spec 10.3 Graphic tab: no second graphic picker and no control for how the second is cut in, in Swiss, Brutalist and Postmodern, and nothing says so.** `src/engine/parts/graphics/swiss.ts:153`, `src/engine/parts/graphics/brutal.ts` (the `controls` line), `src/engine/parts/graphics/pomo.ts` (the `controls` line). The table says "First and second graphic pickers, how the second is cut in, density, scale, line weight, angle". Only Vaporwave gets a second (`vapor.second`). `graphics.cut` is a count (0, 1, 2), not how the second is cut in (spec 8.0: a disc, a column, a band, inside a block; `BRUTAL_CUTS = ['disc', 'column', 'strip']`). The builder's own key list in 10.3 leaves them out and the "Not built" sentence does not name them, so the omission is silent.
Fix: export the cut-in graphic list and the cut shapes from each graphics part (Swiss and Brutalist already roll them from the graphic stream) and generate `graphics.second` and `graphics.cutShape` choices from them; or list them under Carried with a reason. Add them to the lock-rule and the 9.2 tests.

### Should-fix

**S1. Controls shown for a design they do not change.** The test (`tests/advanced.test.ts:42` to `:59`) checks only that each control id changes *some* design (`if (seen.get(c.id)) continue`), not the design it is shown on. The reviewer's probe found, in every one of 6 designs of the layout:
- Swiss `layout.flag` (Graphic: Inset/Bleed) in graphic-above, graphic-below and centred-low on a quarter-turned page: `const out = flag || turned` (`src/engine/parts/layout/swiss.ts:87`) ignores it. The control comes from `FLAG_OF[scene.frame]` (`:39`) with no transform check.
- Swiss type-only: `pick.motif` (Graphic) and `graphics.cut` are shown, but a picked type-only layout draws no graphic (`swiss.ts:47`: only an unpicked layout is moved off type-only).
- Swiss `layout.axis` Col 3 on a tilted or turned page: `axis = Math.min(axis, 1)` (`swiss.ts`, after the size class) makes Col 3 the same as Col 2.
- Swiss vertical: `pos.subtitle`; Postmodern title-side: `pos.info`, `pos.caption` (and `pos.title` in 2 of 6).
- Postmodern Small on a tilted page: `if (size === 'small' && transform !== 'none') size = 'normal'` (`src/engine/parts/layout/pomo.ts:98`) runs after the Advanced value, so the Small segment shows as pressed and the title is drawn Normal. Swiss runs the same line before the Advanced value.
- Brutalist `layout.size` and `graphics.cut` changed nothing in 2 to 4 of 6 designs of title-top, title-bottom, table-first, split, boxed and title-up (no band with those texts).
Fix: hide a control where the plan ignores it (pass `scene.transform` and the layout to the `controls` functions; Swiss flag only untransformed, axis 2 only untransformed, no graphic controls on type-only, positions only where the layout places that slot), move Postmodern's small-under-tilt rule above the Advanced value or hide Small when tilted, and change the test so every control shown for a design changes that design for at least one of its values.

**S2. An Advanced Tilt can drop a Postmodern photocopy design to the fallback layout, and the 9.2 test counts that as a pass.** Probe: seed set 7, `layout.transform = 'tilt'`, every copy-* layout, every size class: 6 of 6 text variants fall back with marks light, 6 of 18 with marks off (seed sets 7 to 9). Seed sets 11 to 14 and 30 to 32 do not fall back. The Done-when asks that either end "still passes 9.2 or the control's range is narrowed until it does". A fallback passes 9.2 but replaces the design the person was tuning. Also, `layout.transform` overrides the style rules that forbid a turn: Postmodern title-side and stairs, a quarter turn in 9:16 (`pomo.ts:70` to `:76`), Brutalist title-up quarter (`brutal.ts:62` to `:63`). The test runs these values over `base(style, 2)` only (`tests/advanced.test.ts:92` to `:103`), not one design per layout, so it does not cover them.
Fix: count fallbacks in the 9.2 test and fail on them; run it over `wide(style)`; let a layout that refuses a transform hide the control's options (or keep the style rule above the Advanced value) and make photocopy hold a tilt, or narrow the options to those that hold.

**S3. Smoke does not run any Advanced value or colour edit through 9.2 with real fonts.** `tools/smoke.py` is unchanged by this build. Every 9.2 claim for hand-set values and colour edits (`tests/advanced.test.ts:92`, `:148`) uses `stubMeasure`. `tools/appcheck.py` taps controls but checks only that the spec and stage change. RUN.md 3 asks the reviewer to look for "an invariant of spec 9.2 that the smoke run does not really check".
Fix: add a smoke pass (`window.__rstr.scene` in the browser) over the generated controls of `__rstr.advanced(spec)` at each value, and over the `editsOf` colour edits, per style x format x mode, reporting fallbacks and unwarned rule 4 failures.

**S4. "(tested)" lines with weak tests.**
- `tests/advanced.test.ts:106` is titled "survives Back, Kept and reload" but never asserts `saved.back`; Back with a hand-set value or a colour edit is not tested anywhere (the appcheck Back steps were not extended). Fix: assert `saved.back[0]` equals `s`, and add a range drag and a colour edit to the appcheck Back steps (one merged step per drag, spec 10.1).
- The finish strength counts as "changes the design" through `strength(s)` in `sig` (`tests/advanced.test.ts:23`). That only proves `strength()` reads its own key. Fix: compare the finished pixels in smoke (strength 0 against 1.5) or a finish unit test on a small canvas.
- `expect(warned).toBeLessThan(n)` (`:167`) passes if all but one edit warns. Fix: record the share and set a floor, for example at least half of the edits repaired without a warning, from a measured count.

**S5. `pick.dialect` sits on the Advanced Layout tab with facet null, so there are two ways in (Look in Simple, Layout in Advanced), and nothing records the move.** `src/engine/parts/layout/pomo.ts` (the `controls` line). Spec 10.3's table does not list the dialect on Layout. The builder's 10.3 line names "Postmodern's dialect" among the keys. That is fine as a choice, but it has to be in the changelog's Choices for Pedro to veto, and the Look tab's reroll row is now split across four tabs. Fix: list both under Choices when the row closes.

### Minor

**M1.** The pattern's recorded fill keeps the palette's mix (`src/engine/parts/ground/pomo.ts:37`, `mixHex(P.ground, P.ink, alpha)`), while the drawn pattern is the *edited* ink over the *edited* ground. Rule 4 reads `fills` (`src/engine/invariants.ts`, `behind`). Ice-cream text sits on full plates, so `behind` returns before it reaches the pattern today, but the check reads a colour that is not drawn. Fix: either map the mix again after the edit (recompute from the edited roles) or record the drawn colours.

**M2.** Range readout `num()` (`src/app/App.tsx:52`) shows two decimals. Small parameters (pattern pitch 0.025 to 0.125, logarithmic) read 0.03 to 0.13, and the slider is linear on a log range. Fix: show three significant figures; step log ranges in log space.

**M3.** `withStyle` (`src/app/state.ts:75`) clears picks and colour edits but keeps every `params` value, so a Swiss `layout.size`, `type.cap` or `g.dots.pitch` set by hand carries into Brutalist. Spec 3 says params are "per style". Fix: drop facet params on a style change (keep finish strengths and marks allowed), or record the choice.

**M4.** Swatch labels are 10 px (`src/app/app.css:123`). That is below the 12 px used elsewhere for hints. Fix: 12 px, or check them on the phone.

**M5.** `src/engine/controls.ts:216` re-exports `formOf` with an unused import, and a second `human` copy lives in `src/app/App.tsx`. Fix: remove the re-export; import `human` from `controls.ts`.

**M6.** The layout, pairing, palette and Tune steppers show `Auto` while the seed decides, not the layout, pairing or palette drawn (for example Layout `Auto` while the lock reads Copy low). From Auto, Next goes to the first option of the list, not the one after what is on screen. Fix: show the drawn value in the muted unset style (`scene.frame`, the pairing used, `scene.palette`) and step from it.

**M7.** At 360x640 the Colour tab puts four swatches on page 1 and the fifth (photocopy Spot, Vaporwave Window) alone on page 2 with Reset (`screen-360x640-adv-pomo-colour-2.png`, `-vapor-colour-2.png`). The design's colours are split across pages. Fix: let five swatches share one row (each still at least 48 px: 5 x 48 + 4 x 6 = 264 px fits beside the page chips), or put the palette stepper on its own page.

**M8.** At 360x640 the marks-allowed switches shorten "Brackets" to "Bracke..." and shrink "Slashes" and "Barcode" below the others (`screen-360x640-adv-*-style-4.png`). Shortening is legal under spec 10.1, but the ninth switch ("Dots") then fills a whole row alone on page 5. Fix: three switches per row at 360 wide (3 rows of 3), or shorter labels (Brkts is worse; consider Corners).

## Checked and fine

- `Math.random()`: only `src/engine/spec.ts:48` (seed creation).
- No UI code touches a canvas context (`getContext` only under `src/engine/`).
- Shuffle's lock rules: `dropFacet` (`src/app/state.ts`) clears every params key by `facetOfParam`, positions with Layout and `pick.colours` with Colour, unless locked. Finish strengths and `marks.no.*` are kept. The two changed tests in `tests/app.test.ts` used `g.copier.grain` as a stand-in for "a key Shuffle keeps". Under spec 10.3's first bullet ("A value set by hand survives Shuffle only if its facet is locked") a `g.*` value must go with Graphic, and the copier and the pattern draw from the graphic stream. So the tests were changed because the spec says so, not to fit the code. They now use `finish.grain.strength` and `marks.no.grid` as kept keys and `g.sun.slits` as a cleared one, and `tests/advanced.test.ts:76` checks every generated control against every lock and reroll.
- Colour edit (`src/engine/recolour.ts`): applied after the layout and the marks, so no text box moves (tested at `tests/advanced.test.ts:127`). A plate goes under the first earlier text item it would cover, so it is never over text (rule 3). Rules 1, 2, 3 and 5 hold for every edit in the test. Every rule 4 failure is in `colourWarn`, and the Colour tab shows "Low contrast: text kept on a plate". No silent failure was found in the test sample or the probe. Repairs use only the design's own colours (`cands`). The edit lives in `pick.colours` per mode, survives `serialize`/`restore`, Keep and `migrate`, is cleared by Shuffle and New colours without the Colour lock and by a palette or style change, and is kept with Colour locked. Palettes are not written (tested).
- The cropped title is still not built, spec 9.2 rule 1 is untouched, and Carried R10 (from R8) still stands, rightly: it needs the rule 1 waiver from Pedro or Fable.
- Carried R10 (from R6) Paper edge on the Type tab, (from R7) strength dial and marks allowed on the Style tab, (from R9) the Vaporwave keys generated from `SKY_OBJECTS`/`FLOORS`/`SECOND`, and the R4 M4/M6 item (six tabs, full pairing name on Type) are built.

## Verdict

**fix.** 2 errors, 5 should-fix, 8 minor. The six tabs, the lock rules, the colour edit and its repair and the persistence are sound, and the scripts pass. The row is not done, though. The builder wrote five spec 10.3 parameters out of the spec on a reason that does not hold for most of them (E1). Spec 10.3's second-graphic and cut-in controls are silently missing (E2). And the "every control changes the design" and "either end passes 9.2" tests can pass with controls that do nothing on the design they are shown on, and with designs that drop to the fallback (S1, S2).
