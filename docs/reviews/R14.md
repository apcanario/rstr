## Errors

**E1. Smoke fails: the Advanced "title size Small" check taps the value the design already draws.** `tools/appcheck.py:272`. The failing spec (smoke log line 192: swiss 9:16 light, graphic-above, seeds 2153899532 / 705816167 / 3268632420 / 2363837860) gives the same pixel hash unset and with `layout.size: small` (`5cc875b3`). With `normal` and `oversize` it changes (`9d995c30`, `53561719`). So the control is not broken (spec 10.3 is kept: it changes the design). The test is wrong: the design comes from a random Shuffle, so the check fails whenever auto already picked small. Commit `825fb37` saw the failure and only made it print the spec. CLAUDE.md says never push with smoke failing. Fix: read what the design draws (`window.__rstr.advanced(spec)`, the control's shown value, or `solve` with each option) and tap a size class different from it, as R10 did for title Low. Then rerun smoke until it passes; if sw-hanken (Carried R10) fails again, rerun and record it.

**E2. The 30-shuffle variety test would pass if the crop never varied.** `tests/photo.test.ts:91-104`. It keys on role plus the drawn rect `x, y, w, h` rounded to 10 px. That rect moves whenever the layout seed moves the box, so with a constant scale and offset (a broken crop stream) almost every shuffle is still "distinct". The roles check (at least 3) catches only a constant role. Fix: key on what `photoChoice` decides: role, `scale` (`params.scale`, rounded to 0.05) and the offset as a share of the room (`(box.x - d.x)/(d.w - box.w)` and the same for y, rounded to 0.05, or 0.5 where there is no room). Keep at least 10 distinct per style. Add a negative check: with `photoChoice` forced to a constant scale and offset (pass `place: { scale: 1.5, x: 0.5, y: 0.5 }` and vary only the role), the count must fall below 10.

## Should-fix

**S1. In Brutalist, and in some Swiss and Vaporwave designs, the photo barely shows or does not show at all.** `src/engine/photo.ts:157-178` (`photoSpot`), `src/engine/styles/compose.ts:35`. Legal, but the photo is the feature. Probe (b), 24 seeds each, share of the photo's box that changes when the photo is drawn (plates count as changed, so the real share is lower):
- Brutalist: `box` median 0.12, 17 of 24 under 25%; `disc` 0.12, 18 of 24; `band` 0.06, 17 of 24; `column` 0.32, 11 of 24.
- Swiss: `disc` 7, `column` 6 and `band` 7 of 24 under 25%; `ground` 0.0 at 9:16 light layout seed 20938; `column` 0.0 at 3:4 dark seed 155561.
- Vaporwave: `band` 0.0 at 9:16 dark seed 92209; `disc` 7 of 24 under 25%.

On the sheets: `photo-brutal.jpg` cells 2, 5, 7 and 11 (3:4 title-top box, 3:4 title-bottom band, 1:1 title-top band, 3:4 title-top column) show a sliver or nothing. The Brutalist band sits under a title that fills the page, so `box` and every cut-in shape in that band are covered by type and plates. `photo-roles.jpg` cell 1 (swiss 1:1 ground): a full-bleed ground photo covered entirely by the Swiss fields, because `photoSpot` lifts a ground photo only over `rect` items that cover the canvas, not over graphics that do. `photo-vapor.jpg` cell 7 (1:1 title-sky band): no photo visible.

The rule that makes it: spec 8.9 lists the roles but sets no floor on how much of the photo shows. Proposed spec line, for Pedro to veto: "Wherever it sits, at least 35% of the photo's box shows once text, plates and the graphics over it are drawn; a placement that shows less tries the next role from the layout stream; `ground` lies over every ground-layer item and every graphic that covers more than 80% of the canvas." Then measure the share on the Scene (photo quad minus the text and plate quads and the opaque graphics above it), use it in `photoSpot`'s choice, and test it in `tests/photo.test.ts` over 100 seeds per style and role, plus a smoke pixel check like probe (b).

**S2. With a pinned role the 9.2 runs skip some text variants.** `tests/photo.test.ts:60` (`n: 4`, start 0: all-1, all-3, all-8, title-alone only) and `tools/smoke.py:351` (`n: max(4, N // 40)` = 5 from start 300: caption-alone, all-1, all-3, all-8, title-alone). No pinned role is ever run with subtitle alone or info alone. Spec 9.2 asks for each slot alone. Fix: `variants: 'all'` with a smaller `n`, or `n` at least 7 (a multiple of the 7 variants), in both places.

**S3. The no-image-data network test has gaps.** `tools/appcheck.py:841` and `:926`.
- `pg.on('request')` does not see requests the app's service worker starts itself. Listen on the context (`ctx.on('request')`), including service workers.
- A GET to this host or the font hosts may carry anything in its URL. Fail on any URL over 2 KB, and on any query string to this host.
- WebSockets are not checked. Fail on `pg.on('websocket')`.
- The flow never exports with a photo, though export is the other path that handles its pixels. Export once inside `run_photo` before the check.

**S4. Photo memory is never released.** `src/engine/photo.ts:45-47`, `src/photos.ts:49`. Every photo that `ensurePhoto` decodes stays in the registry as a full-size `ImageBitmap` (up to 5000 x 5000). That includes every photo in Back (30 steps) and every Kept thumbnail opened. `forgetPhoto` is never called. Twenty different photos at 5000 x 3750 are about 1.5 GB of bitmaps on a phone. Fix: keep the bitmaps only for the current design and the Kept page shown (`forgetPhoto` the rest after each draw), or decode the stage copy at most 3240 px on the long edge and decode full size only for a print export (R16). Test: a unit test of the eviction rule, and an appcheck count of `__rstr.photo` registered ids after Back x 10 with different photos.

**S5. `prune` is not really tested.** `src/photos.ts:67`, `src/app/App.tsx:332`. The appcheck line "the first photo left the phone while Back still holds it" (`tools/appcheck.py:924`) passes whatever `prune` does, because a photo added this session is in `fresh` and is never pruned. Nothing tests that an unreferenced photo is deleted, or that a photo held only by Back or Kept survives a new session. Fix: in `run_photo`, after Remove, reload (so `fresh` is empty) and wait past the 3 s debounce. The photo held by Back must still be stored. Then clear Back (or load a design without a photo over 31 commits) and reload: it must be gone.

**S6. The Advanced "Down" value shows the across offset on any square box.** `src/engine/controls.ts:183`. `room` decides x or y with `d === b.w`. For a disc, and for any square box, `b.h === b.w`, so `sy` is computed from `P.x`. The stepper then shows the wrong value until set. The value written is right. Fix: pass the axis explicitly (`room('x')`, `room('y')`). Test: on a pinned disc with `x: 0.2, y: 0.8`, the controls' `shown` values are 0.2 and 0.8.

**S7. The pixel check of text over the photo hardly exercises the measured path.** `tools/smoke.py` photo pixels: 5 of 251 items measured, 246 plated. The unit test needs only `measured > 0`. Spec 9.4's "text over the photo passes contrast measured on pixels" is checked on 5 items. Fix: add specs that put text over the test photo's calm sky and hills (pin the role `ground` with `scale: 1` and offsets that put dark hills under light text), and require at least 50 measured items read on pixels, none under spec 5.

## Minor

**M1. Advanced tab labels touch at 360 wide.** `src/app/app.css:86`. With seven tabs each button is 48 px. "GRAPHIC" is shrunk to 10 px against 11 px for the rest (fit), and spans 109 to 155 in a 108 to 156 button; "COLOUR" spans 204.5 to 251.4 in 204 to 252. So LAYOUT, GRAPHIC and COLOUR read as one run ("LAYOUTGRAPHIC" in `screen-360x640-adv-photo-1.png`). Legal (no overflow, 48 px), but clumsy. Fix: drop the letter spacing to 0 at seven tabs and keep at least 3 px of padding each side, so every label sets at one size. Or shorten to icon plus a 4-letter label only at under 380 px.

**M2. The crop button reads "Crop" without the "New" caption the other rerolls carry.** `src/app/App.tsx:571-573`. Spec 10.2 calls it New crop. The caption slot shows the pixel size instead. Fix: label it "New crop" (fit shrinks it), or put "New" over it as `newRow` does and move the pixel size to the add row.

**M3. Two plate helpers.** `src/engine/photo.ts:188` (`plateUnder`, pad 0.16 x size, at least 6) and `src/engine/recolour.ts:70` (`plateFor`, newly exported by this row, pad 0.12, at least 4, used only inside `recolour.ts`). Fix: one helper with the pad as a parameter; drop the needless `export`.

**M4. The pixel check of text over the photo leaves out the copier pass.** `src/engine/pixels.ts:324`. Photocopy text over a photo is not read after the pass. R15 brings "the copier pass over a photo": carry it there so R15 reads text pixels with the pass on.

**M5. The test photo is decoded at grid size.** `src/api.ts` `photo.test`: 768 x 1024, then drawn as 3000 x 4000. So the pixel check never meets detail finer than the contrast grid, which is where `MEASURE_MARGIN` 1.15 could be too small. Carry to R16 (2x, 3x export): read text pixels over a full-size test photo at 3x.

**M6. No test of a colour edit on a design with a photo.** `src/engine/recolour.ts` keeps photo items and re-checks with `behind()`, which reads the measured regions, but nothing tests it. Add one case to `tests/photo.test.ts`: a measured design, an ink edit that fails against the region, and the result plated with rule 4 passing.
