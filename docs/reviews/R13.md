## Done-when, line by line

## Findings

### Errors

None.

### Should-fix

**S1. Parts are coupled through `Plan.vals`, an untyped bag the import rule cannot see.** `src/engine/parts/types.ts:54` (`vals: Record<string, number | string | boolean>`). Each type, ground and graphics part reads private keys its own style's layout wrote: `parts/type/swiss.ts:21-24` (`any`, `axis`, `mod`, `G`, `size`), `parts/type/brutal.ts:92,108,139` (`M`, `R`, `ms`, `gap`, `rh`, `flip`, `hot`), `parts/type/pomo.ts:19` (`titleTop`, `ang`), `parts/type/vapor.ts:18`, `parts/ground/vapor.ts:9` (`hz`), `parts/graphics/vapor.ts:14` (`hz`, `sunX`, `sunR`, `sy`), `parts/graphics/swiss.ts:34,56` (`mod`, `M`). That is reading another part's internals by key name instead of by import. Spec 8.7 says parts meet only through the Scene and that the split exists for the mix of 8.8; with this Plan, Swiss type under Brutalist layout returns no text at all (`plan.vals.any` is undefined, `swiss.ts:21`) and Swiss graphics under any other layout draw nothing (`plan.boxes.graphic` absent). Fix: replace `vals` with a typed, shared vocabulary in `parts/types.ts` that every layout fills and every part may read (margin, module, gutter, axis, size class, title-band side, horizon, title area), each with a meaning independent of the style; move style-private numbers (Brutalist `R`, `ms`, `rh`, `hot`) into the part that uses them. Add a test that composes every layout part with every type, ground and graphics part (`compose5` with mixed `Parts`) over a few seeds and asserts each returns text items or `null` and never throws or silently drops all text; the import rule stays.

**S2. The range-end test of 9.2 cannot fail.** `tests/graphics.test.ts:50-68` and `tools/smoke.py:69-84`. A graphic's parameters never change anything `check()` reads: the box, quad and fills are set at placement and every draw is clipped to its box (`paint.ts`, `paintGraphic`), so 9.2 on the Scene passes at any value. Two further gaps: `reached > 0` (line 65) passes if 1 of the 18 specs carries the value and the other 17 fell back and dropped the graphic; and the smoke pass uses 2 seed sets per end (`smoke.py:75`) with `variants: 'cycle'`, so each end meets one text variant per spec. Fix: (a) make the test pixel-level, which is what an extreme can actually break: at each end, render the Scene and the same Scene with its graphics removed, and assert the pixels inside every text item's box are identical (the `knockLeak` machinery in `src/engine/pixels.ts` already does this for marker colours; generalise it), in smoke; (b) assert `reached === n` minus the specs where the style cannot place that graphic (count and assert `fallbacks === 0`); (c) run every variant (`variants` unset) at each end.

**S3. Swiss has drifted from its approved refs, and the drift is not recorded.** `shots/refs-compare.jpg`: `swiss-bars-3x4` (wide stripes became dense thin ones), `swiss-dots-1x1` (graded fine screen became uniform large dots), `swiss-blocks-9x16` (black block, red block and disc became a red block and a black bar, no disc), `swiss-notext-1x1` (hairline contours became thick bands). Cause: parameters are now drawn from the graphic stream (`parts/graphics/swiss.ts:40`), so the ref seeds give new values; `tools/refs.py:52` pins transform, size class and cut count but no parameters. Spec 8 says the `refs/swiss-*.jpg` and `v2-swiss-*` files are the target, and R5's Done-when needs the first approved look to stay reachable. Fix: give each Swiss entry in `tools/refs.json` the `g.<graphic>.<param>` values that reproduce the approved look and pin them in `refs.py`; if the blocks ref's disc cannot be reproduced with `graphics.cut: 0`, pin the cut that reproduces it. Record the drift in the changelog. If the fixer cannot reach a ref, carry it to R5 by name.

**S4. New logic without unit tests.** `src/engine/parts/frame.ts` (`frameFor`, `bleed`), `src/engine/geom.ts` (`compose`, `unapply`), the combination rule in `parts/graphics/swiss.ts:47-85`, the transform and size-class draws in `parts/layout/swiss.ts:108-116`. Smoke shows the outcomes are legal but no test pins the rules themselves. Fix, in a unit test on the stub measure: `unapply(xf, apply(xf, p)) === p` and `compose` against two `apply` calls; for each format, `frameFor('tilt'|'quarter')` gives a page whose transformed corners lie inside the canvas (and inside 260..1600 in 9:16); `bleed` grows only edge-touching sides and the result covers the canvas after the transform; over 600 shuffles of Swiss, tilt and quarter each fall in 1/6 +- 0.05, the tilt angle in 9..14 degrees either way, cut-ins 0/1/2 near 0.38/0.52/0.10, every cut shape and every plate role appears, and `block` only inside `blocks`; small, normal and oversize titles differ in size on the same seeds.

**S5. No committed guard that Postmodern, Brutalist and Vaporwave stay unchanged.** The builder's one-off comparison and this review's (3600 Scenes, identical pixels) prove it for this commit only. R5 edits shared code those three styles draw through (`graphics/*`, `paint.ts`, `parts/frame.ts`, `styles/compose.ts`, `layout.ts`), and their rows (R6, R8, R9) come later. Fix: add a snapshot test on the stub measure: for each of the three styles, a hash of the Scene (items' kind, quad, fill, name, params) over e.g. 40 seed sets x 3 formats x 2 modes, compared with a committed value; a row that changes a style on purpose updates its hash and says so in the changelog.

### Minor

**M1. Brutalist item order changed** (`parts/type/brutal.ts:168` with `styles/compose.ts`: band graphic now before the caption/info/subtitle rules and the hot-line plate, was after them). Not a behaviour change: the band and every rect are disjoint (band ends a full `gap` before the title area; the plate reaches `gap/2` past it), pixels identical in 1200/1200 Brutalist Scenes above, and 9.2 rule 3 only looks at items after a text item, all of which are unchanged. The row's "no behaviour change" holds. Fix: none to the code; the changelog should say the order changed and that pixels were compared.

**M2. Style-specific code sits in `parts/shared/`, which the import rule treats as neutral.** `parts/shared/brutal.ts` (title lines and the stretched block) is imported by both Brutalist layout and type, so layout typesets the title to size the band (`parts/layout/brutal.ts:45`). It is pure and is how 9.3 keeps the band fixed, so it is legal; but any style can grow a `shared/<style>.ts` channel the same way. Fix: allow `parts/shared/` only files that no single style owns, or name the exception in the test (`tests/parts.test.ts`) with a comment saying why.

**M3. The import-rule test does not exercise `export ... from` or dynamic `import()`** although the regex handles both (`tests/parts.test.ts:11`). Fix: add one case of each to "the rule catches each kind of breach".

**M4. The vitest variety run only logs** (`tests/graphics.test.ts:88-94`): it asserts `count === 300` and nothing else. Fine for this row by its Done-when; when R5 enforces Swiss, assert `pass` there, not only in smoke.

### Legal but clumsy (Swiss, for R5's tuning pass)

- Tilt with the small title class leaves the title tiny and half the poster empty ground: swiss, 3:4, light, seeds `{layout: 72452, colour: 847032, graphic: 10406972, type: 9648}` (`variety-swiss.jpg`, row 2, cell 1); swiss, 3:4, light, `{layout: 159561, colour: 1999051, graphic: 24703771, type: 9989}` (row 3, cell 4). The tilt shrinks the page (`frame.ts:170`) and `small` takes 0.55 of that (`parts/type/swiss.ts:15`); do not allow `small` with `tilt`, or raise small's share when the page is scaled.
- A disc cut-in half hidden under the full-bleed text panel reads as an accident, not as depth: swiss, 3:4, light, `{layout: 24938, colour: 218658, graphic: 2608718, type: 9462}` (`variety-swiss.jpg`, row 1, cell 3). Keep cut-ins out of `plan.boxes.panel` or let them cross it by at least half (`parts/graphics/swiss.ts:51-53` uses the whole page as V for full-bleed).
- A band cut-in whose disc graphic is clipped flat by the band's lower edge: swiss, 3:4, light, `{layout: 56433, colour: 735103, graphic: 9100963, type: 4217}` (`sheet-swiss-light.jpg`, row 2, cell 2). Same cause as Carried R5 (disc radius up to 0.72 of the short side, `graphics/disc.ts:9`).
- 9:16 with the small class: title and info set small high on the canvas with a wide empty field beside the column graphic: swiss, 9:16, light, `{layout: 40595, colour: 525645, graphic: 6501545, type: 4155}` (`sheet-swiss-light.jpg`, row 1, cell 6).

Postmodern, Brutalist and Vaporwave sheets match their refs in `refs-compare.jpg`, as expected for an unchanged build. No text cut or wrapped on any control; every control at least 48 px (smoke's app checks and the screens looked at).

## Verdict

**fix.** Errors 0, should-fix 5, minor 4 (plus 4 clumsy Swiss seeds for R5). Every Done-when line is true as written, but S1 and S2 mean two of the tested lines are weaker than they look.
