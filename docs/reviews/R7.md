## Questions the main chat asked

- **Two-pass rule (spec 7.1):** implemented as written. Pass one paints everything but text and the surface pass (`finish.ts:377`); then the effect; then text and the copier pass (`finish.ts:387`). Grain and Grunge cover everything, then the title is stamped again at 0.5 and 0.55. Correct by the letter. The plates under text are "graphics" and get the effect, which is what breaks legibility in E1.
- **2/255 tolerance:** the line says "the same". The real worst case is 1/255 on four stroke-edge pixels (above). It hides no effect: the weakest effect on a pixel (scanline at 0.90) moves a channel by far more than 2. Still, tighten it (M1).
- **Guard and "same pixels":** consistent. The guard copies Clean's pixels back over the item and its ring (`finish.ts:312`), so ink stays Clean's under VHS and Glitch, and the result is deterministic. Under Grain and Grunge a put-back item becomes an unfinished patch the shape of the item and its ring (3 items in smoke); that is a visible trade the changelog should state (M6).
- **Brutalist with fewer than three marks or none:** spec 7.2 says light is "three or four marks", in free space only. "Free space only" decides where a mark goes. It does not excuse a quarter of a style getting none. Measured: Brutalist at light gets 0 marks in 153 of 600 designs (25.5%) and 1 or 2 in 11; the other styles always get 3 or 4. The test allows 35% under three (S1).
- **No date in micro labels:** allowed. Spec 7.2 lists what a label *may* carry; it requires none of them. A render-time date would break spec 3's "the same spec draws the same design". Record it under Choices (M6).
- **150 ms tile delay, stale renders:** no stale-render race. Each tile is drawn synchronously, the `stop` flag is checked before each one, and the effect's cleanup sets it, so a tile of a design already left is never drawn. The delay only defers the first tile, though. Each tile then blocks the main thread for a full 1080-wide render with finish and guard (S3).
- **Finish and Marks at 360 x 640 and 412 x 915:** fit. At 360 x 640 the finish buttons are 51.6 x 48 and the marks buttons 68.7 x 48; at 412 x 915 they are 62 to 104 wide and 52 tall. `elementFromPoint` at every centre hits the button; labels fit unshrunk past their box; nothing is cut by the screen. `appcheck.py` also hit-tests every button at its centre on every page at both sizes and taps each Finish and Marks value.
- **`Math.random()`:** only `newSeed` in `src/engine/spec.ts:40`. The finishes use xorshift and `rng` seeded from the graphic seed; marks use the layout seed.
- **Snapshot hashes:** unchanged and verified. `tests/snapshot.test.ts` is not in the diff and passes with the old hashes for all four styles. Off returns the Scene untouched (`marks.ts:255`), and `stepLight` returns the same pairing object when nothing is light (`layout.ts:96-103`; the test proves no pairing steps).
- **Logic without tests:** `restore()` is exercised only in smoke (3 put back). `corruption`, `smearOf` and the restamp have no test of their own. `stepLight` has no check that the stepped weight exists in the family (M7). The E1 and E2 fixes need the tests named there.

## Findings

### Errors

**E1. Glitch moves text plates out from under their text, and letters vanish.** `src/engine/finish.ts:377` paints plates (the Vaporwave info window, the ice-cream highlight plate and info box, the Brutalist subtitle bar) in pass one. `glitchKernel` (`finish.ts:177`) then slides bands 10 to 90 px sideways. The text is drawn back in place over a plate that has moved. In `shots/finish-glitch.jpg`: Vaporwave 9:16 dark, info reads "E: LISBOA" and "TION: 01", because "WHER" and "EDI" now sit in dark ink over dark ground. Ice cream 3:4 light: the "A" of "A modular identity system" sits on the plate's black shadow and cannot be seen. Postmodern 1:1 dark: the "W" of "WHERE" is off its label. Brutalist 3:4 dark: the subtitle bar is displaced under "A MODULAR". VHS's tracking band and head-switching shift do the same on a smaller scale. The guard and the test average each item over its whole ink and ring (`finish.ts:287`), so a few lost letters never pull the mean under 3:1. This breaks spec 7.1 ("a finish never takes a text item under 3:1"; "these effects never touch text").
Fix: (1) After the VHS or Glitch kernel, and before pass two, copy back from a pass-one buffer taken before the effect the pixels inside every text item's box padded by `RING` + 2. A plate then stays exactly under its text, and the effect still runs everywhere else. Alternatively, move every rect or graphic that lies under a text item's box into pass two. (2) Make the guard and `finishProof` local: measure each item in cells (per run, or columns about one cap-height wide), and fail or restore when any cell falls under 3:1 that Clean has at 3:1 or more. (3) Add a smoke case on the cells above (seeds from `tools/sheet.py`, n = si*3+fi: Vaporwave 9:16 dark `{layout: 1000+11*7919, colour: 2000+11*104729, graphic: 3000+11*1299709, type: 4000+11*31}`) that fails today.

**E2. Grain and Grunge turn the photocopy title words grey, and nothing checks it.** `shots/finish-grain.jpg` and `finish-grunge.jpg`, Postmodern 1:1 dark and 9:16 dark: "AND NOISE" and "Signal AND" are crisp white with a dark edge in `finish-clean.jpg`, and pale grey ghosts with a light rim under both finishes. Probe: Postmodern 3:4 dark, marks light, seeds `{layout: 8932, colour: 106729, graphic: 1302709, type: 4031}`: `title.word1` goes from 1.46 under Clean to 1.14 under Grain and 1.15 under Grunge (mean ink against ring). The guard puts back only items that Clean has at 3:1 or more (`finish.ts:343`). `finishProof` reports only those (`pixels.ts:214`). So every colliding photocopy word is exempt, and the finish may take it anywhere. The likely cause is the restamp (`finish.ts:372`). It repaints the title TextItem, with its paper halo and pre-copier fill, at 0.5 over the copier-passed word, so un-copied colours are laid half over the copied ones. The fixer should confirm by rendering with `RESTAMP` at 0. This breaks spec 7.1 ("no colour ghost on type") and 8.2 ("readable by default").
Fix: restamp from the Clean render masked to the title's ink, as `restore()` already does, at part strength. It is then in register and in exactly Clean's colours, copier pass included. For items Clean reads under 3:1, the guard and `finishProof` must still forbid a drop: require finished ratio ≥ 0.95 × Clean's ratio, and put the item back otherwise. Add the seeds above to smoke.

### Should-fix

**S1. Brutalist gets no marks in a quarter of designs, and the test allows it.** Stub measure, 600 designs per style at light: Brutalist 0 marks in 153, 1 or 2 in 11. Swiss, Postmodern and Vaporwave get 3 or 4 every time. `shots/marks-light.jpg`, Brutalist 3:4 dark: no mark at all. Cause: `free()` (`src/engine/marks.ts:56`) needs `M` = 40 px from the edge plus 18 px from text. Brutalist's 50 px margin, full-width title and ruled table leave no such spot (1:1 with a 3 or 8 word title, 3:4 with 3 words). `tests/marks.test.ts:43` accepts up to 35% of designs under three, pooled, so it passes. Spec 7.2: light is three or four marks.
Fix: add flush edge candidates: rulers on the canvas edge at x 0 or W − 22, status and barcode in the band between the last rule and the bottom edge. Allow a gap of 10 px for marks 24 px thick or less. Count the 9:16 story zones as free for marks, as spec 3 allows (today `busy` only penalises them). Then test per style: at light, no design with 0 marks and at most 5% with fewer than three.

**S2. The same mark twice.** `marks.ts:270` loops `kinds.length * 2` times and places a kind again once each has been tried. Brutalist has three kinds and draws four marks half the time, so it always repeats one: 272 repeats in 600. Swiss: 129. `shots/marks-light.jpg`, Brutalist 1:1 light: two identical barcodes, both "SEED 024F-24F8", and two status rows stacked beside the title; the same in `marks-full.jpg`. It reads as a mistake.
Fix: each kind at most once per design, with `n = min(n, kinds that fit)`. Brutalist then draws three. Test: no `params.kind` twice among the free marks of a Scene.

**S3. Style tiles with a finish block the phone for seconds.** `src/app/App.tsx:300-322`: the 150 ms delay defers only the first tile. Each tile is a synchronous full 1080-wide render, finish and guard included (`src/engine/index.ts`, `thumb`), and each blocks the main thread. Under a 4x CPU throttle (closer to a phone than desktop Chrome): Grunge tiles take 572 to 1064 ms each, 3.6 s for four; Glitch 1.5 s; Clean 0.45 s. A Shuffle tap during a tile waits for it. Carried R7 asked for a phone measurement, and only desktop numbers were taken.
Fix: draw tiles with `finish: 'clean'`, since a tile shows the style, not the finish. Alternatively, apply the finish at tile size: the kernels already scale with `u`. Record the throttled numbers in the changelog.

### Minor

**M1. The ink test allows 2/255; the line says "the same".** `src/engine/pixels.ts:210`. The only differences found are 1/255 on stroke-edge pixels of the Vaporwave chrome title, where the combined fill, stroke and echo mask is full but the stroke's coverage is not. Fix: build the ink mask from pixels that the fill alone covers fully (paint the mask with stroke and echoes off), require exact equality there, and drop the allowance. Otherwise keep 1/255 and put it in the changelog's Choices with the reason.

**M2. The label test reads params, not what is drawn.** `tests/marks.test.ts:72`. Today `drawMark` writes only `params.label` and `label2`. A future mark that draws its own string would pass. Fix: paint the Scene into a recording context that captures every `fillText` string drawn by mark items, and check each against `labelOf` of an allowed source set.

**M3. Glitch slices wrap round the canvas.** `finish.ts` `glitchKernel` uses `wrap(x - shift, W)`, so a band shifted right brings the right edge's content in on the left (visible on Swiss 1:1 in `finish-glitch.jpg`). `elements.md` says "shifted". Fix: clamp to the edge pixel, or fill the gap with the row's own shifted copy. Pedro may prefer the wrap; if kept, note it under Choices.

**M4. Corruption bars avoid text only.** `finish.ts:256`. `elements.md` puts them "in empty areas". Today they land over graphics and plates (Brutalist 1:1 `finish-glitch.jpg`, bars across the table). Fix: also reject boxes that overlap a graphic or rect item's quad, with the current 6 tries; fall back to fewer bars.

**M5. VHS and Glitch can move mark pixels onto text.** Marks are painted in pass one, and slices shift up to 90 px with marks kept only 18 px from text. A label or barcode fragment can land between title letters. Not seen in the sheets; untested. Fix: paint marks after the effect, in pass two before text. Alternatively, the E1 copy-back with a pad of 90 px for mark pixels.

**M6. For the changelog at close:** the export timings (9:16, Grunge and Glitch, every style, copier pass alone; this review's re-measure agrees); Choices: no date in micro labels (spec 3), the guard's put-back patch under Grain and Grunge, the 150 ms tile delay, counter fixed at `01 / 01`; the stage itself under a 4x throttle takes 1.46 s per change with Grunge (Clean 0.14 s). Add a By-hand line for Pedro to say whether Shuffle lags on the phone with Grunge on.

**M7. `stepLight` does not check the stepped weight exists.** `src/engine/layout.ts:101` adds 100 to any weight under 400. No pairing has one today, so it changes nothing. A pairing with a 300 face would ask `fonts.ts` for a face that may not exist. Fix: step to the next weight listed for the family in `pairings.json`, or leave it; test it against the list.

## Verdict

**Fix.** 2 errors, 3 should-fix, 7 minor. The engine, the two-pass order, the marks layer and the controls are sound. Two Done-when lines are not true as the sheets show them (E1, E2), and the test meant to catch them measures too coarsely to fail.
