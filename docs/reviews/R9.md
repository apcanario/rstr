## Findings

### Errors

None.

### Should-fix

**S1. The 60-shuffle pass is a lucky seed, not a property.** The builder's remark that the pass is thin and rests on the order of graphic-stream draws is right. The key the test counts (layout, transform, first graphic, second graphic, palette) is drawn independently on every shuffle. Its distribution gives about 3 expected repeated pairs in any 60 (786 distinct keys in 1200 shuffles).

Measured over 20 consecutive windows of 60 (shuffles 0 to 1199, `shuffleSeeds(i)`, stub measure), repeats per window:

| Run | Repeats per window | Windows with 0 |
|---|---|---|
| Vaporwave, every format | `[0,3,4,1,1,1,0,3,1,1,1,1,0,1,1,0,2,2,4,1]` | 4 of 20 |
| Swiss 3:4 | | 5 of 20 |
| Brutalist 3:4 | | 6 of 20 |
| Postmodern 3:4 | | 17 of 20 |

So the test passes only because the window it reads (0 to 59) happens to be clean. Any change to how many graphic-stream draws a design takes (a new extra, a reordered `rg()`) moves every later decision and can flip it. The same holds for Swiss and Brutalist, which R5 and R8 passed the same way.

The key also does not depend on format: the three format runs produce the same 60 keys. So "passes in 1:1, 3:4 and 9:16" is one test run three times (`tests/graphics.test.ts:131`, `src/engine/proof.ts:186`).

Fix:
- The spec line (8.0, "no two designs share…") is a property of Shuffle, so hold it there. `shuffle` in `src/app/state.ts:31` keeps the variety keys of the last 59 shuffles in the session. When the new design's key repeats one, it redraws the unlocked seeds, up to 8 tries.
- `runVariety` (`src/engine/proof.ts:211`) runs through the same guard.
- Add a test that runs 20 windows of 60 from different seed bases and asserts 0 repeats in each, for every style.
- This spans styles: carry it as a cross-style item (R12 or a new row) if it is not fixed here, and say in the changelog that the R5, R8 and R9 passes were seed-dependent.

**S2. The third graphic of spec 8.0 is skipped, and `graphics.cut` means something else in Vaporwave.**
- `src/engine/parts/graphics/vapor.ts:95-98` and `:112-115`, `:145`: one cut-in at most, on `uc < 0.6`. Spec 8.0: "about six rolls in ten cut a second graphic into the first… About one in ten adds a third." Swiss (`parts/graphics/swiss.ts:95-96`) and Brutalist (`parts/graphics/brutal.ts:71-72`) draw 0, 1 or 2 (`u < 0.1 ? 2 : u < 0.62 ? 1 : 0`).
- In Vaporwave `graphics.cut = 2` draws one, so the shared Advanced key gives different results by style.
- The edited spec 8.4 is silent on the third.

Fix:
- Take the shared rule: `drawn = typeof cutSet === 'number' ? clamp(round(cutSet), 0, 2) : u < 0.1 ? 2 : u < 0.62 ? 1 : 0`. The second cut is a smaller disc or a band offset inside the focus disc, from `VAPOR_CUTS` minus the first cut.
- Add the third to the spec 8.4 graphics line.
- Test the shares (about 60% and 10%) and `graphics.cut` 0, 1 and 2.

**S3. Gradient bars read as a fence.** `gradbars` has `angle` 0 (across) or 1 (down), and the graphic stream picks it. Drawn down behind the sun or the sky object (`graphics/vapor.ts:107`), they stand as vertical posts either side of the disc. Seen in:
- `shots/sheet-vapor-light.jpg` and `-dark.jpg`, cells 4 (1:1 mint title-band) and 5 (3:4 lagoon title-window);
- `shots/variety-vapor.jpg`, row 1 cell 1 (title-band, sphere) and row 2 cell 6 (title-floor, checker disc);
- the busiest-sky and gradient-bar sheets of this review (mint light 3:4 title-floor: four posts and two rails round the sun).

The genre's bars, and the spike's, run across behind the sun, thinning toward the horizon.

Legal but clumsy; the spec that makes it: vapor, 3:4, light, mint, title-floor, `params {"vapor.second": "checker", "graphics.cut": 1}`, seeds `{layout: 23857, colour: 203, graphic: 3899427, type: 493}`, title "Signal and noise in the long summer of light".

Fix: at `:107` pass `{ angle: 0 }` (and `prog` at least 0.5, bars thinning downward), so the bars behind a sun always run across. Keep `angle: 1` for the field layouts' second graphic (`:137`), where a column of bars reads as a backdrop, not a fence.

**S4. The Advanced keys and the cut share have no tests.** `vapor.first`, `vapor.second`, `vapor.extras` and `graphics.cut` (`graphics/vapor.ts:87-96`) pin the refs (`tools/refs.json`). The new tests (`tests/vapor.test.ts`) and the refs check do not assert what any of them does. Nothing asserts the "about six rolls in ten" cut share either. A change that broke them would show only as a drifted `refs-compare.jpg`, which a person must notice.

Fix: in `tests/vapor.test.ts`, over every horizon layout and 40 seed sets, assert:
- `vapor.first: 'sphere'` gives `scene.graphics[0] === 'sphere'`;
- `vapor.second: 'checker'` gives the floor `checker`;
- `vapor.extras: 0` gives no `mountains` and no `gradbars` unless picked;
- `graphics.cut` 0 gives no `cut === 'disc'` item and 1 gives one;
- unpinned, the cut share is 0.5 to 0.7.

**S5. The 9:16 sky title is small over a dead band of sky.** Legal but clumsy. `layout/vapor.ts:84-87` sizes the title box to 47% of the horizon and sets `pos.title = 'top'`. The sun is sized to sit under the whole box, not under the text (`:85`). At 9:16 the box runs from y 260 to about 560, so the title is smaller than at 3:4 on a taller canvas. Between the subtitle and the sun lie about 260 px of empty sky.

The spec that makes it: `shots/ref-vapor-9x16.jpg` (the `vapor-9x16` ref of `tools/refs.json`: s 3, miami, dark, title-sky). The subtitle ends near y 530 and the sun starts near y 790. In `refs/vapor-9x16.jpg` the subtitle meets the top of the sun. The busiest-sky 9:16 sheet with the 8-word title shows the same in all 16 palette forms. 3:4 has a smaller gap (about 100 px) and 1:1 none worth noting.

Fix: set `pos.title = 'bottom'` for title-sky (`:87`). The block then sits at the foot of the box, a gap above the sun. The empty sky moves above it, under the story zone, where the stars are, as in the ref. Every line still sits inside the 47% box the contrast claim covers. Then re-read `ref-vapor-9x16`.

### Minor

**M1. Rings and dots floors are flat.** `FLOORS` (`graphics/vapor.ts:24`) includes `rings` and `dots`, drawn flat into the floor box (`:117`). A floor of huge concentric rings reads as a target, not a ground plane. Seen in `shots/sheet-vapor-light.jpg` cell 3 (9:16 lagoon) and cell 10 (1:1 miami), and in this review's 9:16 layouts sheet (title-floor, title-window). Fix: squash them by the floor's perspective (scale y by the checker's `curve`, centre on the vanishing point), or keep them for the field layouts only.

**M2. Star fragments beside the title.** The knock-out clips stars that cross a glyph halo, leaving small ticks and L-shapes next to the letters. `shots/ref-vapor-3x4.jpg` near (230, 185) and (697, 188). Fix: in the knock step, drop a star whose box meets any hole (`graphics/vapor.ts:162`, or in `graphics/stars.ts` read `avoid`) rather than clipping it.

**M3. Gradient bars twice in one design.** `cutName` excludes only `first` and `second` (`graphics/vapor.ts:95`), so with `barsOn` the cut-in can be gradbars as well: busiest-floor sheet, miami light, `dots+checker+stars+gradbars+gradbars`. Fix: also exclude `gradbars` when `barsOn`.

**M4. The orb's second graphic covers its lower half.** `rest` for orb starts at the sphere's centre (`graphics/vapor.ts:124`), and the second is drawn after the first (`:144`). Gradbars or a checker run over half the sphere (this review's 9:16 layouts sheet, orb light and dark). Fix: start `rest` at `f.y + f.r * 0.8`, or draw the second first for orb as for windows (`:143`).

**M5. The Advanced keys are loose ends for R10.**
- `vapor.first` is ignored in the field layouts and loses to `pick.motif` (`graphics/vapor.ts:88-90`), and it duplicates part of what `pick.motif` does.
- `PARAMS_OF` (`src/app/state.ts:21`) lists none of the four keys, so Shuffle would not clear them once a control writes them.

Fix: say in spec 8.4 which wins, and add the keys to Carried R10 (graphic facet in `PARAMS_OF`, controls generated from a list the part exports).

**M6. The layout-count test is weaker than its name.** `tests/vapor.test.ts:18` asserts at least 3 layouts without a horizon under a title that says four. Fix: `toBe(4)`.

**M7. Marks full never underlines a title on a window or band.** 39 of 108 titled designs (title-window, windows, title-band) get no bar. That justifies the floor of 69, but spec 7.2 says full gives the title an underline bar. Fix: let the bar sit on the title's own plate, under the title (`src/engine/marks.ts:351`, treat the title's plate as free for the bar), or record the exception in spec 7.2.

**M8. Untested logic.** These have no direct test; only the invariants pass over them:
- the sun clamp to a third off the side (`layout/vapor.ts:113`, the Carried Mint item);
- the huge sun giving way to the text (`:159-163`);
- the title size classes (`VAPOR_SIZE`, `type/vapor.ts:75`; in the sky box the fit clamps most of the difference away).

Fix:
- Assert `sunX - sunR >= -sunR / 3` and the mirror over every horizon design.
- On sun-huge, assert the title box is at least `want` high.
- At title-floor, assert the oversize median title px exceeds normal, and normal exceeds small.

**M9. The record is not updated in the build commit.** `README.md`, `docs/BACKLOG.md` and `docs/CHANGELOG.md` are untouched. Fix at the close:
- README "What it does today" and Status;
- the R9 changelog line with this review's counts;
- clear Carried R9 and R9 (from R13);
- close the R1 refs line;
- carry S1 across styles if it is not fixed here, and M5 to R10.
