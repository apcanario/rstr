## Questions the main chat asked

- **Copier pass exempt from rule 3:** allowed. Spec 8.2 puts the pass "over everything including type" and 8.7 makes it the ground's pass. A graphic over text is what rule 3 forbids; this is texture by definition. Allowed too: leaving it out of `graphicLeak`, which asks whether a *graphic* paints inside text. What is lost: nothing now checks what the pass does to type. Spec 7.1's 3:1 floor is written for finishes, but 8.2's "Readable by default" plainly asks the same of the pass. S3.
- **Pixel check accepting blends of three colours:** it weakens the check, and for every style, not just photocopy. S2.
- **Photocopy title words overlapping each other under rule 2:** allowed. Spec 8.2 says "negative leading so words collide", and the group is checked against every other slot. What the exemption leaves open is how much one word may hide another. S1.
- **`Math.random()`:** only in `newSeed` (`src/engine/spec.ts:40`), and `tests/random.test.ts` enforces that. The copier's xorshift is seeded from the graphic stream. Clean.
- **Paper edge on the Look tab, not cleared by Shuffle:** while Advanced has no tabs of its own (R10), the Look tab is a fair place for the switch. Leaving the value through Shuffle is not fair now that the switch exists. It also stays in force, unseen, after Advanced is switched off. S5.
- **Snapshot hash:** only `pomo` changed (`8627a7e7` to `310e2f55`). Swiss, Brutalist and Vaporwave hashes are unchanged, so the shared changes (`invariants.ts`, `compose.ts`, `pattern.ts`, `proof.ts`, `block.ts`) move nothing in the other styles' Scenes. Expected and correct; the changelog at row close must say so (M10).
- **Smoke flake (stub-host "unused sw-hanken"):** not reproduced. 10 of 10 isolated runs passed (about 2 s each), and it passed in this smoke run. The failing message was not recorded, so it is unknown which of the four conditions on `tools/fontcheck.py:153` failed. The only read in that branch with no wait is `note(pg)`, which reads `[data-testid="font-note"]` once, right after `idle()`. Preact normally commits `setNote` before `idle()` returns, so the app has no race I can show. A harness race is possible: the read has no wait and a loaded machine could defer the commit. Not shown to be an app race. M8.

## Findings

### Errors

**E1. The ghost copy is hidden under the paper halo.** `src/engine/parts/type/pomo-copy.ts:262` offsets the ghost by (0.05, 0.045) em, about 0.067 em. `src/engine/paint.ts:48-55` draws echoes, then the halo stroke, then the fill. The halo is `lineWidth = 2 * width` with `width = 0.06 em`, so it reaches 0.06 em past every glyph edge and covers all but about 0.007 em of the ghost: one pixel at a 150 px word. In `shots/ref-v2-grunge-2-3x4.jpg` the "and" ghost shows only as hairline blue slivers. In `shots/refs-compare.jpg` none of the six photocopy cells shows a ghost; every grunge ref shows one plainly ("Signal" in yellow, "AND" in blue). Spec 8.2: "one has a ghost copy in the spot colour". The test (`tests/pomo.test.ts:152`, `ghosts > 50`) counts Scene echoes, not pixels, so it passes.
Fix: put the ghost clear of the halo. Offset it 0.10 to 0.16 em (graphic or layout stream, both axes), or give the ghost its own paper halo under it, so it reads as its own copy. Keep it in the quad (it already is). Add a pixel test with paper edge on: render the hot word with and without its echo and require at least 2% of the word's ink-box area to differ, in the spot colour, outside the halo mask.

**E2. On a 360 x 640 screen the Postmodern Look tab has a control that cannot be tapped and one that is cut.** Simple and Advanced both. Five or six Look rows make three pages, and the chips then take two columns (`src/app/app.css:48`, `.chips.many`, 102 px), which narrows the rows to 216 px. Measured with `elementFromPoint` at each button's centre: New **Type** (left 229, right 277) sits under chip 3 and cannot be tapped. **Glitch** (222 to 270) is cut to "Gl" under chip 1 or 3. Glitch is disabled until R7, so the cut label matters less; New Type is a live Simple control. Spec 10.1: "Text on a tile or button never wraps and never overflows" and controls at least 48 px. The smoke passed because `LAYOUT_JS` (`tools/appcheck.py:39-63`) checks each element only against the panel's outer box, and the hidden buttons lie inside it, under the chips. The check has no test for a control covered by a sibling or clipped by a row.
Fix: keep the chip column one 48 px column. On a short screen with more than two pages, either (a) make it a 48 px page stepper (one chip showing `1/3`; a tap goes to the next page) or (b) keep the Look tab at two pages by sharing a row: Light/Dark and Dialect on one row, each a 3-way or 2-way segment. Then add a hit test to `LAYOUT_JS`: for every shown `.app button`, `document.elementFromPoint` at its centre must be the button or inside it, and its rect must lie inside its parent row's rect. Run it on every page of the Postmodern Look tab in Simple and Advanced at both sizes. Today `appcheck.py:275` and `:295` check only the page that holds the Dialect or Paper edge row.

### Should-fix

**S1. Title words can bury one another; nothing bounds the collision.** Rule 2 skips pairs in the `title` group (`src/engine/invariants.ts:103-104`), and `copyTitle` places words with `LEAD = 0.78` and random x (`pomo-copy.ts:32`, `:241-244`) with no limit on cover. The sliced word fares worst: its lower half shifts and the next word runs over it.
Legal but clumsy:
- `ref-v2-grunge-5-3x4`: pomo 3:4 light, seeds `{layout: 277166, colour: 3665518, graphic: 45489820, type: 1}`, pick `{palette: terrazzo, dialect: photocopy, layout: copy-stack}`, params `{layout.transform: none}`. "AND" is a strip of its top third under "NOISE".
- `shots/variety-pomo.jpg` cell 23 (`shuffleSeeds(22)`, 3:4, copy-column): "AND" is in fragments.
- `shots/sheet-pomo-photocopy.jpg` cell 1: pomo 1:1 light, seeds `{5100, 5200, 5300, 5400}`, dialect photocopy, copy-cover. "Signal" is half hidden under "AND".

The refs collide by about a quarter of a word's height and never hide a word.
Fix: after placing each word, measure how much of the previous word's ink box (its height in the overlapping x span) the new word's ink box covers. Above 0.35, raise the new word's baseline until it is under 0.35, inside the binary search's height so the stack still fits. Add a stub test over 200 photocopy specs: no title word has more than 40% of its ink box under later title words.

**S2. The pixel check's three-colour blend is global and loose.** `src/engine/pixels.ts:107-112` now accepts any pixel within 6 of the triangle of any three declared colours, for every style. With ground, ink and accent declared, almost any mixed colour on that plane passes. A real leak in Swiss or Vaporwave that blends toward those colours would no longer be caught. The changelog will say this was added for photocopy's many opaque pieces.
Fix: accept the triangle only where three areas really meet. Require the `withG` render's 3 x 3 neighbourhood of the pixel to hold at least two distinct declared colours other than the nearest one. Or limit it to Scenes with `plan.dialect === 'photocopy'` (a flag on the Scene). Add a test with a pixel of a fourth colour inside the triangle of three that must still count as `stray`.

**S3. The copier pass darkens title words at the page edges, and no check covers it.** `src/engine/graphics/copier.ts:26,35-37`: each side darkens with chance 0.55, across 2% to 7% of the width (up to 76 px at 1080), down to black. Title words may run 3% off the page (`pomo-copy.ts:201`, `wcap = pg.w * 1.03`), and the text area's margin is 72 px. So the first or last letters of an edge word and its paper halo go dark. Example: `shots/text-pomo.jpg`, 8 words at 1:1: the "E" of EIGHT sits in the dark edge. Raised contrast (up to 1.3) and dust also act on type. That is allowed: the pass is meant to be rough. Spec 8.2's "Readable by default" and 7.1's floor ("a finish never takes a text item under 3:1") are not checked for it at all.
Fix: either keep the dark edges outside the text quads (cap `eL`, `eR`, `eT` at the distance to the nearest text quad, read from the Scene the pass is given), or test the floor. Render each photocopy design with and without the pass, and in each title word's fill mask require the median fill against the median halo to stay at 3:1 or more. The test belongs in smoke beside `graphicLeak`, over the 9.2 seeds with the paper edge on. Carried R7 (from R6) already asks R7 to treat the pass like a finish; this is the R6 half of it.

**S4. One title word is sliced only about two designs in three.** `pomo-copy.ts:196` draws `cut` freely; at `:253` the boxed branch returns before the slice. So when `cut === boxed` (1/n of designs: a third for the default 3-word title) no word is sliced. Spec 8.2: "One word is sliced and its halves offset, one sits in an ink box, one has a ghost copy". `hot` is already kept off `boxed`; `cut` is not. The test (`sliced > 50` of about 600 words) cannot see it.
Fix: draw `cut` from the units that are not `boxed` (for n ≥ 2), as for `hot`. Test: every photocopy design with two or more title words has exactly one part ending `b`, one boxed word and one word with echoes.

**S5. The Paper edge value survives Shuffle and stays hidden in Simple.** `src/app/state.ts:21-29` (`shuffle`) and `reroll` leave `params` alone. Spec 10.3: "A value set by hand survives Shuffle only if its facet is locked; otherwise Shuffle clears it." The switch exists from this row on; Carried R10 (from R6) defers clearing it to R10. Worse, switching Advanced off hides the switch (`App.tsx:395`) while the waiver stays in force: Simple then draws rough title words with no control that explains or undoes it.
Fix now, not in R10: in `shuffle`, drop `params['pomo.paperEdge']` unless the Type facet is locked; in `reroll(s, 'type')`, drop it too. The Paper edge is how type is set, and spec 10.3 puts it on the Type tab. Unit test in `tests/app.test.ts`. Narrow Carried R10 (from R6) to the move to the Type tab.

**S6. The "own face, size and case" test cannot fail on size or case.** `tests/pomo.test.ts:126` asserts `new Set(sizes).size >= 1`, which is always true; case is not checked at all.
Fix: over the 200 designs, assert at least two sizes in each design of two or more words (with `RELS` this holds unless the width cap equalises them; where that happens, assert it on at least 90% and print the rest). Assert that the words do not all share one case.

**S7. Ice cream tiles: words on one line run together.** `src/engine/parts/type/pomo.ts:188`: `space = px * 0.32` against a letter gap of `px * 0.12`, with tiles rocking. Two words on a line read as one, e.g. "ANDNOISE":
- `shots/sheet-pomo-icecream.jpg` cell 7: pomo 1:1 light, seeds `{layout: 52614, colour: 633574, graphic: 7803554, type: 5586}`, dialect icecream (title-side, lilac).
- Cell 8: 3:4 dark, `{60533, 738303, 9103263, 5617}` (title-side, milano).

The ref `pomo-9x16` puts one word per line.
Fix: a word gap of at least `px * 0.75`, or break the tile title one word per line where `lines.length` stays at 3 or fewer.

### Minor

**M1. Shuffle keeps `pick.dialect`: a reasonable choice that contradicts the letter of spec 10.1.** Spec 10.1 says Shuffle clears every unlocked facet's picks, and 8.2 makes the dialect a layout-stream pick. A Dialect control that Shuffle resets would be useless, so the choice is sound. But the spec should say it. Per RUN §2: add "dialect" to the spec 3 list of settings Shuffle never changes (and a line in 10.1), in the fixer's commit.

**M2. Shortened labels on the 360 px Dialect and Paper edge rows.** "Ice cre…", "Photoc…" and the hint "On: title words h…" are legal (shrink, then shorten) but the hint says nothing at that width. Fix: hints of at most 14 characters ("Holds over ink" / "Rough, no check"), or drop the hint below 380 px.

**M3. A test name claims more than it checks.** `tests/pomo.test.ts:78` says "every text item sits on a plate"; nothing asserts it. Either assert it (each ice cream text item's `behind()` is a rect of its own slot) or drop the clause.

**M4. A picked layout of the other dialect is silently ignored.** With `pick.dialect = photocopy` and `pick.layout = 'stairs'`, `parts/layout/pomo.ts:52` draws a random photocopy layout. Fix: a picked layout wins over a picked dialect (or the picker lists only the dialect's layouts, for R10); test it.

**M5. No screenshot of the Postmodern Look tab.** `tools/sheet.py` saves only Swiss Look pages, so E2 could not be seen from `shots/`. Add `screen-<size>-look-pomo-<page>.png` in Simple and Advanced.

**M6. Smoke never runs 9.2 with the paper edge off in the browser.** Only 30 stub specs (`tests/pomo.test.ts:155`) do. Add a smoke pass: pomo photocopy, `pomo.paperEdge: false`, the 9.2 text variants, rules 1, 2, 3, 5 all held.

**M7. The 9.3 pixel check does not pin photocopy.** `tools/smoke.py:134-140` draws 4 seed sets per format with the dialect left to the stream. The copier pass reads and writes canvas pixels, the most likely place for nondeterminism, and may never be drawn there. Fix: run it with each `pick.dialect`.

**M8. The flake.** See above. Fix: in `tools/fontcheck.py:151`, wait for `[data-testid="font-note"]` (5 s) before reading it, and print the full `d`, note and pick on failure (it does) into the changelog when it fails. Record the one failure in the changelog's flaky line as the builder reported it.
