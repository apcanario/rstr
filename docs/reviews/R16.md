## Findings

### Errors

None.

### Should-fix

**S1. Print files do not say they are 300 dpi.** Files: `src/engine/png.ts:67-74`, `src/engine/export.ts:219-227` (`blobOf`).
- Spec 10.1 calls Print "A3 at 300 dpi". But `PngWriter` writes no `pHYs` chunk, and the canvas encoders (`convertToBlob` / `toBlob`) cannot set a density.
- So a print shop or desktop app opens the 3508 x 4961 file at its default of 72 or 96 dpi, which is roughly 1.2 m tall, not A3.
- Fix: for size `print`:
  - write `pHYs` (11811 px/m both ways, unit 1) in `PngWriter`, and splice the same chunk after IHDR into canvas-encoded PNGs;
  - patch the JPEG's JFIF APP0 density to units 1, 300 x 300 (bytes 13 to 17 after the SOI, inserting an APP0 if there is none);
  - for WebP, say on the sheet that it carries no print size, or leave WebP out of Print.
- Test: `exportcheck` reads `Image.open(...).info['dpi'] == (300, 300)` for Print PNG and JPEG on all three paths.

**S2. The honest failure can offer the size that just failed.** Files: `src/engine/export.ts:225,251,260`, `src/app/App.tsx:286-289`.
- `ExportError.largest` is always `null`. The sheet works out "largest that works" itself with `workingSizes`, and that only asks `canMake`.
- When a size fails for another reason, `workingSizes` still lists it. Examples: `toBlob` gives null on a big canvas, the draw runs out of memory, then the strips-into-canvas encode fails too and a JPEG ends in `ExportError`.
- The sheet then says "largest that works: Print" about the Print it just failed on, and `setSize` puts it back. Spec 10.1 says the sheet "offers the largest that works", and this offer is false.
- Fix: keep a set of sizes, per format and kind, that failed in this session and leave them out of `workingSizes` (and so out of `expOk`, which greys them). Set `largest` in `makeExport`'s errors from that, and have App read `e.largest`.
- Test: add a `Limits` hook (for example `failEncodeAbove: <px>`) so a whole canvas can be made but its encode fails. Check that the error names a smaller size and that the failed size is greyed after.

**S3. The photo pixel line has no test and compares widths only.** Files: `src/engine/export.ts:276-285` (`photoNeed`), `src/app/App.tsx:668`.
- Row R16 lists "the photo pixel count", and no tool or test calls `photoNeed` or reads the line.
- `n.has[0] < n.wants[0]` decides "drawn anyway" on the width alone, so a photo short of what the size wants in height only is not flagged.
- Fix: flag when either axis is short (`has[0] < wants[0] || has[1] < wants[1]`).
- Test: in `exportcheck`, register `full-portrait` (3000 x 4000) as ground at scale 2.5 in A, and check that `photoNeed` at Print gives the cover rect at Print scale (`wants` equal to the photo box times 3508/1080 and 4961/1527) and that `has` is shorter. In `appcheck`, open the sheet with that photo at Print and check that `export-info` says `drawn anyway`.

**S4. The sheet is not checked at its fullest at 360 x 640.** Files: `src/app/App.tsx:657-671`, `src/app/app.css:137-138`, `tools/sheet.py`, `tools/appcheck.py` (`run_limits`).
- At 360 x 640, Print with bleed already fills the sheet up to the stage (`screen-360x640-sheet-export-print.png`, two info lines).
- `expLines` can hold five lines: the note, "cannot make", the size, sRGB, and the photo line (about 70 characters, for example `Photo 3000 × 4000 px; this size draws it at 7017 × 9356, drawn anyway`). `fit` shrinks a line to the width, so a long line can drop well under 12 px.
- No screenshot or layout check covers a photo with a failure note. Spec 10.1 says nothing scrolls and text never wraps or overflows; the scripts cannot yet say whether this holds.
- Fix:
  - shorten the lines (for example `Photo 3000×4000; needs 7017×9356` and `Print: sRGB, colours print duller`);
  - fold the "cannot make" line into the note when both are there, so there are at most four lines;
  - give the hint a floor below which it shortens instead of shrinking.
- Test: run `LAYOUT_JS` and take a screenshot at 360 x 640 with a photo, Print, bleed and `?maxpx=6000000`.

### Minor

**M1. The Brutalist light-marks limit went from 1 to 2: justified, but the test now reads loose.** File: `tests/brutal.test.ts:265-279`.
- The run prints the two designs it lets through: `3:4 c0 quarter` and `A c0 quarter`, both with the same seeds (`layout 1479392985, colour 3433914015, graphic 1222804819, type 1236622662`).
- The A one is the known Carried R12 (from R8) (1) design in the new format: the full-bleed bar blocks the edge strip. So the loosening hides no new defect.
- The test name still says "at most one of 600", and a limit of 2 would also pass a new, different design.
- Fix: rename the test, and allow only that seed set (in any format) plus at most 1 other in 800. Add "also in the A format" to Carried R12 (from R8) (1).

**M2. The error messages use internal size keys, and one contradicts the spec.** File: `src/engine/export.ts:251,260`.
- They read `This phone cannot make print for A` and `...; PNG can`. Spec 10.1 says "PNG may", and the sheet's own line says "PNG may".
- Fix: use the labels (`Print`, `3x`) and "PNG may". Export a label map from `formats.ts` so App's `SIZE_LABEL` and these messages share it.

**M3. R16 changes what 1x draws, and nothing records it.**
- The finish kernels and the copier pass now take their noise from `noiseAt` (a position hash) instead of xorshift streams. Unturned treated photos are now treated on the canvas grid (`treatedOnGrid`) instead of `treatedBox`.
- Every non-Clean design, every photocopy design and every treated photo now draws different pixels at 1x than before R16. Kept designs reopen with a different grain pattern (same statistics). No pixel snapshot catches this. The Scene hashes are unaffected, and that is correct.
- Also, a 1x export now goes through `renderSingle` with a `willReadFrequently` context, while the stage goes through `render()` with a plain one. On a phone with GPU canvases the 1x file may differ from the stage by antialiasing. Before R16 both used `render()`.
- Fix: state the pixel change in the changelog. Use one context setting for the stage and the 1x export, and add an `appcheck` step that compares the stage's pixel hash with the decoded 1x PNG of the same spec.

**M4. The PNGs carry no colour-space tag.** File: `src/engine/png.ts:74`.
- Spec 10.1 says "Colour is sRGB". An untagged PNG is usually taken as sRGB, but not by every print workflow.
- Fix: write an `sRGB` chunk (intent 0) after IHDR, together with S1's `pHYs`.

**M5. The PNG writer doubles its output in memory.** File: `src/engine/png.ts:68-73`.
- `end()` copies every compressed part into one `flat` array before cutting IDAT chunks, so the compressed file is held twice. This is on the path meant for low-memory phones; the bench peak there is 736 MB resident.
- Fix: write each compressed part as its own IDAT chunk (or chunks of at most 4 MB) without `flat`.

**M6. Opening the sheet allocates full-size probe canvases.** Files: `src/app/App.tsx:648-652`, `src/engine/export.ts:62-79`.
- `expOk` runs `workingSizes` for every ticked format and size in a `useMemo` on the main thread. Each new size runs `probe`, which makes the whole canvas (up to 3579 x 5032, about 72 MB) and frees it.
- With all four formats ticked that is up to about 10 probes, around 500 MB in turn, the first time the sheet opens. The time is not measured.
- Fix: probe after the sheet has painted (in an idle callback, sizes enabled until known). Add to H4: "say whether the Export sheet lags the first time it opens".

**M7. The sRGB line is not beside Print.** File: `src/app/App.tsx:665`.
- Spec 10.1 puts the line "beside Print". It shows only once Print is chosen, in the info block under File (it is visible in the screenshots).
- This is a reasonable reading. Record it under Choices so Pedro can veto it.

**M8. The builder wrote implementation choices into the spec.** File: `docs/SPEC.md:359`.
- The "Built at R16" paragraph adds decisions the spec did not make: Print takes A alone and ticking another format moves to 3x; the bleed is the trim's edge mirrored, not the ground and graphics drawn past the trim; 512-row bands; JPEG and WebP greyed where no whole canvas fits.
- `CLAUDE.md` treats the spec as settled. Fix: list each of these under Choices in the R16 changelog entry so Pedro can veto. The mirrored bleed matters most: a mirrored photo or gradient reads differently from one drawn on past the trim, though only 3 mm of it shows.

**M9. Strip renders are slow and say nothing while they run.** File: `src/app/App.tsx` (`save`, label `Making…`).
- In headless Chrome, strips take 10 to 20 s at 3x where one canvas takes 3 to 5 s. A3 strips into PNG take 20.8 s. On a phone this can pass a minute with only `Making…` on the button.
- Fix: show the strip count while it works (`Making… 4 of 10`), and add to H4: "time a Print PNG on the phone".

## Clumsy but legal (spec 9.5)

`shots/format-A.jpg` (16 designs, A, light and dark, Clean):
- Cell 1 (A swiss light c0): the waves fill the top two thirds and the title sits low-left, small for the page, with a wide empty band between them. Spec 8.1 allows this layout, and this is the R12 (from R5) "empty" kind.
- Cell 7 (A vapor light c0): the title stands vertical in a narrow window and the page is left lilac and empty to the right, so it reads cramped. This is spec 8.4's window layout.
- Cell 12 (A pomo dark c2, photocopy): "SIGNAL" lies over the top of the script "and". The dialect allows overlaps by halo, as before R16.

All three are of kinds already in Carried for Pedro's eye; R16 adds no new kind. The export-scale sheet shows marks and grain drawn at 3x, crisper and finer than 1x enlarged. The VHS scanlines keep their spacing relative to the page, as they should. The print sheet shows the bleed continuing the edge with no seam.

## Verdict

**fix.** 0 errors, 4 should-fix, 9 minor. All four Done-when lines are true and tested. The fixes are print metadata (S1), the honest failure's offer (S2), the photo line (S3) and the sheet at its fullest (S4).
