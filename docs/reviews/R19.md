## Findings

### Errors

**E1. The 5% guard limit holds only when pooled across styles. Vaporwave fails it, and R19 made that worse.** `tools/smoke.py:350-357`.

What the build does:
- The R19 check adds put-backs over all 400 designs (`pooled`) and fails only on the total.
- Per style, the figures are printed but never tested. Vaporwave: Grain 21/286 (7.3%), Grunge 33/286 (11.5%). Postmodern: Grain 17/587 (2.9%).

Why that is not enough:
- Spec 9.4 gives every finish figure "at strength 1 over 100 seeds per style". Spec 7.1 says the limit is there "so a design is never clean type on a dirty page". About one Vaporwave text item in nine under Grunge is exactly that.
- Pooling across styles makes a failing style pass.

The builder's note (`smoke.py:352-354`) also understates the baseline:
- It cites only R7's Grain on Vaporwave (5.6%).
- The fixture shows R7's Grunge put back 25/286 (8.7%) on Vaporwave, and R7's Grain 16/286.
- R19 raised these to 33 and 21.

Fix:
- Test the limit per style and per finish over the R19 seeds.
- Make Vaporwave's chrome/gradient title hold by itself. Clean reads its cells at 3.0 to 3.1:1, so any grain takes them under. Two options:
  - give it the same `quiet` treatment as small type, and a restamp rim, under Grain and Grunge;
  - or have the Vaporwave type part keep a contrast margin (for example 3.3:1 on every cell) when a finish other than Clean is on.
- If Pedro prefers to accept it, change spec 9.4 to say "pooled over the four styles" and put a Carried item on Vaporwave, with the per-style figures in the changelog.

### Should-fix

**S1. The raised thresholds are not recorded.** `tools/smoke.py:22-27`, `docs/SPEC.md` 9.4.

Five figures were raised to 1.5 x the R7 baseline:
- Grain in all four styles: Swiss 9.62, Postmodern 9.23, Brutalist 9.08, Vaporwave 9.80 (spec: 9).
- Grunge in Swiss: 14.43 (spec: 14).

VHS was not raised, because 1.8 x is above 1.5 x. These numbers exist only as values computed at run time and as console output.

The Done-when asks for a changelog record. Fix: list the five raised figures, with their R7 baselines, in the R19 changelog entry. Add one line to spec 9.4 ("raised by R19 to …") so that a later row does not read 9 and 14 as the bar.

**S2. The committed tool cannot rebuild the R7 fixture or the R7 bench.** `tools/finishbase.py:17,28,54`, `tests/fixtures/finish-r7.json`, `tools/smoke.py:29-30`.

The tool and the fixture do not match:
- `finishbase.py` writes `{styles: {style: {items, grain: [dict…], …}}, glitch, bench}`.
- The fixture holds float lists (`grain`, `grunge`, `grungeMid`, `grungeRest`, `vhs`, `glitch`, `restored`) plus `source` and `seeds`, and has no `bench`.
- Some step turned one into the other, and that step is not in the repo.
- If `finishbase.py` were run again as committed, `smoke.py` would crash on `sum` over dicts.

The R7 bench also has no committed source:
- `R7_BENCH` and the 638 ms throttle figure come from a run that is not in the repo.
- The R7 run that is on record (`/tmp/base.log`, 20:37 on 27 Nov) gave different figures: for example Grunge photocopy 439 ms against 501, Grunge Vaporwave 458 against 376, throttle 754 against 638.

`CLAUDE.md` says the record is part of the product. Fix:
- Have `finishbase.py` write the fixture's exact format, `bench` included.
- Have `smoke.py` read `R7_BENCH` and the throttle baseline from the fixture.
- State in the changelog that the R7 build was measured twice and how far apart the two runs were.

**S3. Grunge's tone step takes far more than a fifth of the saturation from light colours, so acid and pastel grounds go to paper.** `src/engine/finish.ts:331`.

Spec 7.1 step 1 says colour "keeps its hue and loses about a fifth of its saturation". The code uses `sat = 0.8 * (1 - q*q)` with `q = (2c-1)²`. That keeps 0.8 only at mid-tone and falls to 0 toward both ends of the curve. A colour at luma 0.85 (Brutalist acid yellow, Postmodern mint, Vaporwave chrome) keeps about 23% of its saturation.

You can see it on the sheet:
- Brutalist 3:4 dark: the acid yellow title and plate turn pale cream.
- Postmodern 1:1 and 9:16 light: the mint ground turns near white.
- Vaporwave 3:4 dark: the yellow in the sun stripes is lost.
- In each case the style's palette identity goes.

Fix: use `sat = 0.8` across the range. Only the clipped ends (`c` at 0 or 1, toner and paper) should go neutral. If Pedro wants light colours to wash out as on a real copier, say so in spec 7.1.

**S4. The 1x/3x test reads the plan, not what is drawn.** `tests/finish.test.ts:281-305`.

The test would still pass if `grungeKernel` or `vhsKernel` stopped using `grungePlan`/`vhsPlan`, or drew more streaks or bands at 3x (for example if a count came from `H` or `u`).

Fix: run the kernel itself with one term on (`feed` for Grunge, `tracking` for VHS), on a flat grey picture 1080 px wide and again 3240 px wide. Count the runs of changed columns or rows and require the same count at both sizes.

**S5. Grunge's `quiet` mask is a spec change that the spec does not show.** `src/engine/finish.ts:823-858`, used at `finish.ts:349-354`.

What the mask does:
- Under and around every text item: no uneven toner.
- Under and around small text: no speck, pinhole, dust or hair.

This is a reasonable way to meet the 5% limit, and the doc comment gives the measured reason. But spec 7.1 steps 2, 4 and 5 say "the whole page", and step 7 relies on specks showing inside letters. Large type still gets them.

Fix: add one sentence to spec 7.1 Grunge saying what holds back on type and why, and put it under Choices in the changelog.

**S6. Grunge against `refs/v2-grunge-*.jpg`: where it falls short.** Every point here is legal and is a taste call for Pedro. In each case the spec says which rule makes it.
- **Pinholes read as a starfield, not toner.** On dark solids (Swiss 1:1, Postmodern 3:4 and A dark, Brutalist 3:4 and A, Vaporwave dark) the dropout is an even scatter of crisp paper-white dots. The refs' blacks are a dense fine grey grain with few holes. On Vaporwave the dots merge with the style's own stars. Made by spec 7.1 step 4: pinholes in 1.2% of dark pixels, 1 to 4 px, lifted to full paper. Spec change to propose: pinholes 0.5% at 1 to 2 px, lifted half-way, clustered by the toner noise. The 18-level grain should carry the solids.
- **No ruled feed lines.** The refs (all six) show thin horizontal lines every 30 to 40 px across the whole sheet. That is what reads as "copier" on light paper. Ours draws 5 to 9 isolated streaks, mostly vertical, and 2 to 4 bands. Made by spec 7.1 step 3. Proposal: add a faint regular line pattern along the feed (1 px, 3 to 5 levels, about every 36 px at 1080).
- **Light paper is flat.** On light designs (Brutalist 9:16, Vaporwave 1:1 and 9:16, Postmodern 9:16) the paper is a clean flat 240. The refs' paper carries visible grain and a warm tint. Made by step 1 (the lightest 12% sent to one value) and step 4 (18 levels of grain, cut back by the tone weighting). Proposal: paper at 232 to 240 under the uneven-toner noise, with grain 22 levels.
- **The edge shadow is narrower than the refs'.** Refs 5 and 6 darken all four sides with a soft falloff about 6 to 8% of the width deep. Ours darkens 1 to 3 sides, 24 to 60 px, with a harder inner edge. Made by step 6. This is closer to the refs than R7 was; Pedro to say.
- **Pastel and acid grounds go white or cream.** See S3.
- **It does read as a copy now.** The tone, streaks, dust, hairs and the lid shadow put marks across the whole page. It is no longer R7's ragged border.

### Minor

**M1. The Grunge interior figure is tested on the mean.** `tools/smoke.py:346`. Spec 9.4 says "on every one" for Grain but not for Grunge, so testing the mean is a legal reading. Still, 22, 21, 15 and 25 seeds per style miss it on their own (printed as "seeds short"). Fix: write the reading under Choices in the changelog, or test it per seed with an allowance (for example at most 10%).

**M2. Nothing tests the wiring of the second generation.** `src/engine/finish.ts:882`. The unit test checks `grungeTerms(scene)`. If `finishBody` stopped passing `gt` to the kernel, nothing would fail. Fix: a smoke check that a photocopy design under Grunge differs from the same design drawn with `grungeTerms` forced to `{}`. Or export one function that builds the kernel's arguments and test that function.

**M3. The 2x export-time limit is printed, not checked.** `tools/smoke.py:372`. Fix: warn (not fail, since it is timing) when `total / was > 2`, and write every figure, plus the throttle figure, into the R19 changelog entry. On this run Grunge photocopy at 9:16 took 750 ms, above R7's recorded 688 ms but under 2x.

**M4. Per-pixel noise does not scale at 3x (pre-existing since R7).** `src/engine/finish.ts` `grainKernel`, Grunge's grain (step 4) and VHS's raw streak noise are one value per export pixel. At 3x the grain is a third the size, which goes against 7.1's "every length scales with the canvas width". The counts are right; the texture is finer. Fix: sample `noiseAt` on a lattice of `u` px (`Math.floor(x / u)`) in all three, or record this as an accepted exception in 7.1.

## Verdict

**Fix.** E1 (Vaporwave over the 5% limit, hidden by pooling) and S1 to S5 must be dealt with in this row. S6 is Pedro's call on the refs. The rest of the row is built as specified and holds up:
- Glitch is pixel-identical to R7.
- The terms are separable.
- Strips match the single canvas (0 values differ).
- All the 9.4 brightness figures pass.

Counts: 1 error, 6 should-fix, 4 minor.
